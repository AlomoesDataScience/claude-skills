## What it is (Claude's summary)

A grim extraction roguelike: you run mercenary operations from your ship, choose a mission for a
faction, send a cloned operative into a procedurally built station, fight turn by turn with
detailed wounds and status effects, and either extract with loot or lose what the body carried.
Between missions you trade, craft and upgrade the ship, while an otherworldly threat (the
quasimorphs) grows.

## For our games

Wounds-and-effects combat and the extraction loop are close to the user's Heresiarch (tactical
roguelike); many factions with their own wars in one solar system fit the 4X engine's
"factions inside one system" and subfaction play.

# Field of Glory II Medieval - play reference (read BEFORE every battle)

Read from the manual for playing, 2026-11-02 (ch. 8-18, 23, 28-31; printer-friendly PDF pages in
brackets). My words. The user: "go read the manual to learn to play, literally."

## Seeing the battlefield (ch. 8)
- Grid squares, one unit per square, 8 facings. Diagonal move = 1.5x AP. **G** toggles the grid.
- Fog of war: squares without line of sight are darkened; yellow ? marks terrain edges that may hide
  enemy. Woods, buildings and hills block LOS. Woods/buildings/marsh hide stationary foot.
- Camera: W/A/S/D scroll, F/R zoom out/in (or wheel), Q/E rotate. Minimap button toggles top-down
  vs isometric (isometric recommended for play; top-down for reading the field).
- **Banners tell everything** (8.5.2, 15): design = side; banner direction = unit facing; big banner =
  general (C-in-C: gilded ball on top); tattered cloth = losses; upper part **yellow = Disrupted,
  red = Fragmented, whole white = Routed**; **my banners darken once the unit has moved**.
  **H** (or Settings > More Options) switches side B to **Distinct** banners - use it to tell sides apart.
- Left panel = selected unit; right panel = hovered unit (AP, type, quality, armour, capabilities,
  cohesion). **Ctrl+left-click** a unit = detailed info incl. all its POAs vs each enemy type.
- Top left: % of each side routed/dispersed and the difference (green winning, red losing).
- Tools menu (right edge triangle): Next Unmoved (Tab), Next Unshot (B), Line of Fire (1), Line of
  Sight (2), Command Range (3), Detailed Unit Info, Combat Log (C), Casualties (K), **Unit List (L)**:
  moved units greyed, "Can Shoot" yellow, "Melee Due" orange, click to select.
- End Turn top right, then Confirm; clicking elsewhere cancels. **End Turn is the user's.** Esc = menu.

## Force selection and deployment (8.2, 10, 11.9, 11.15, 11.17)
- Fixed core units + optional ones up to the points limit (depends on difficulty). Left-click a
  list entry adds, right-click removes (also right-click a bought unit on the map with nothing
  selected). Autofill, Autodeploy exist - don't accept the default line blindly.
- Custom battles: "Preview Enemy Options" next to Accept - look at it.
- Stakes / field fortifications can be toggled off to save points.
- Deployment: drag/move/swap units in the highlighted area (lights get a larger area), set facings.
  Keep units near their general. **Switch Command** and **Dismount/Remount** (D units) only here.
  Add General: small armies with <3 generals can add some (not to artillery/baggage).

## Generals and command (ch. 9)
- Commands each have a general. Command range: Troop Commander 4, Field Commander 8, Great Commander
  12 squares (toggle with 3). Checked at the START of my turn and fixed for the turn.
- Out of range ("Reduced CC"): no free 45 deg turn (can still move 1 square with a 45 deg turn).
- C-in-C commands anyone; sub-generals any non-allied troops; ally generals only their own command.
- General in a fight: +50 POA to his unit, +1 to cohesion tests of friends within range/4 squares;
  but he can't command while fighting, and may be killed (likelier if losing) -> cohesion tests for
  friends within 1 square (sub) / 2 squares (C-in-C, ally).
- Move General: once per turn to a unit of his command within 4 squares (not out of melee/pursuit).
- Rallying: a unit with a general always tests to rally; without, much rarer. Put generals on
  wavering units behind the line.

## Movement (ch. 11)
- Open ground 4 AP straight, 6 diagonal; other terrain costs more for some types.
- **ZOC**: primary = square in front; secondary = the two squares beside that. You may enter, not
  pass through. Already in a ZOC: only move away (within 45 deg of directly away) - mostly lights.
  Non-light troops ignore lights' ZOC. Red highlight = primary, yellow = secondary.
- Move Whole Command (blue triple arrow) only if no unit of the command is within 5 squares of enemy.
- Turns: moving within 45 deg of facing is free. In range non-light, non-unmanoeuvrable: one free 45
  deg turn per turn. Lights: one free turn any direction. Else 45 deg = 4 AP, 46-90 = 8 AP, more = all
  AP. => **foot can't turn >45 and move; horse can turn 90 and still move 1-2 squares.**
- Unmanoeuvrable: knights, undrilled heavy foot, warriors, cataphracts, elephants, artillery, large
  units (10+ models), raw/untrained units.
- In an enemy primary ZOC you can only turn to face a flank threat or another enemy whose primary ZOC
  you're in.
- Fall back 1-2 squares keeping facing; inside charge reach of non-light enemy = cohesion test
  (Fragmented units will probably break).
- Light foot pass through friends only straight front-to-back.
- **Undo** = last move only; not after a charge or shot, not if the move revealed unseen enemy, not
  after a fall back that caused a test.

## Shooting (ch. 12, 11.11; tables p.65-67)
- Ranges (short/long, long = half effect): foot bow, longbow, crossbow 2/4; mounted bow & crossbow,
  sling 2/-; javelins 1/-; foot handgun 1/2; heavy artillery 6/9; light artillery 6/-.
- Arc: within 22.5 deg = full; 22.5-45 = half the men; beyond 45 = can't. Target icon: three arrows
  = full arc, one arrow = half. **Turn and Shoot** exists as one order.
- Must be ordered (no automatic fire). Shooting blocked by units except through chequerboard gaps;
  bows/crossbows/slings can shoot over units only from higher ground.
- **Ammo: 5 good shots**, then half effect. Hold fire at long range unless several units hit the
  same target. **Stationary shoots better**; moving or turning lowers it. Artillery can't after moving.
- Effect vs armour (POA, vs foot): bows/javelins vs Protected -75, Armoured -150 (longbow -125,
  crossbow -125), Heavily Armoured -225 (longbow -175, crossbow -155), Fully -250 (longbow -191,
  crossbow -165). **Crossbow and longbow are the armour-killers**; bows/javelins barely hurt heavy
  armour. Skirmisher targets -100, cover up to -225, pursuers -150, quality -50..+100.
- Massed bowmen/crossbowmen without any melee ability get nervous within 2 squares of enemy combat
  troops facing them (-70) - keep them out of contact.
- 100 POA ~ 33% more/less casualties.

## Close combat (ch. 13, POA tables p.69-70)
- Charge: needs AP to enter the target square (not actually moved in); target within 45 deg of front
  at the start; free 45 turn allowed; Charge icon clicked TWICE (first shows Win:Draw:Lose; Ctrl =
  detail). In a primary ZOC you may only charge that enemy (or one whose primary ZOC you're in) - plus
  the square directly ahead if in its secondary ZOC.
- Light foot can't charge unbroken non-lights unless the target is in rough/difficult terrain.
- **Evasion**: lights and non-lancer cavalry may evade (AI decides by odds); lights always evade
  non-light chargers unless in rough/difficult terrain or behind an obstacle. Chargers pursue with
  remaining AP (+-4 AP random; later knights always get +4). Evaders lose some men; off-map evaders
  count half routed. A charge's pursuit may switch to another enemy met on the way.
- **Flank/rear**: counts only if the charger starts its whole move behind the target's flank (tooltip
  says so). Vs an unengaged unit: min net +50 POA. Vs a unit already in combat or that evaded this
  turn: it drops a cohesion level automatically and impact is fought at net +200 (unless lights vs
  non-lights, foot vs mounted, mounted vs elephants).
- **Impact**: only men in contact fight; then **melee** every turn until one side breaks/breaks off
  (Fight Melee anytime in my turn; unresolved melees resolve at End Turn anyway).
- Combat strength: only first 2 ranks of foot (8 models), 4 models of horse; mounted have 33% more
  per model, but a standard 8-model foot unit has 50% more than a 4-model horse unit (+25% in melee).
  Fighting 2+ enemies: -20% strength per extra enemy (max -50%) -> **gang up**.
- Net POA difference capped at 200; 100 POA ~ 33% modifier. Key POAs (see SKILL.md "Reading a unit
  card" for the full list): knightly lancers +200 vs foot / +100 vs mounted at impact, OPEN TERRAIN
  ONLY; steady defensive/offensive spear, pike reduce lancer bonus; spearmen +100; heavy weapon +100
  (cancels 50 of enemy armour); better armour up to +100 vs foot in melee; general +50; higher ground
  +25 / +100 (>=100 height); foot behind obstacle +25, field fortifications +100, heavy +200.
- "Open terrain" combat needs both units in open and no obstacle between.
- Steady = not Disrupted/Fragmented/Broken and not disordered.
- Push backs and follow ups: foot shock troops (impact foot, offensive spearmen, pike) that initiated
  the fight follow up a pushed-back foot enemy - may carry them into a flank charge.
- Break offs: mounted break off from foot if losing prospects; from mounted if lost badly.
- After a combat ends, victors and breakers-off can't move or shoot that turn (45 deg turn maybe).
- In melee a unit not facing its opponent turns to face at its next turn (non-lights won't turn to
  face lights if that exposes them to a flank charge).

## Cohesion (ch. 14, table p.74)
- Steady > Disrupted > Fragmented > Broken (routing). Disrupted/Fragmented fight worse; Fragmented
  move less, can't charge, may break if charged.
- Test (2d6, need 6; Untrained re-roll 6s, Superior 1s, Elite 1s+2s) when: shooting losses >~10% in a
  turn; losing a close combat round; a friend breaks adjacent (2 squares for elephants); a general in
  line of command lost nearby; fall back in charge reach. Fail = -1 level, below 3 = -2 (not from
  shooting; from combat only if lost badly).
- Modifiers: -1 shooting losses >16%; -1 combat damage >5%, -1 more if lost by a large margin; -1
  lost >25%, -1 more >50%; -1 threatened flank (foot battle troops); -1 Disrupted/disordered, -2
  Fragmented, -3 Broken; -1 shot by artillery/handguns; -2 foot losing impact vs knightly lancers in
  open; -1 vs other lancers, vs impact foot; -1 medium/warrior/bowmen/light foot losing to mounted or
  heavy foot in open. +1 heavy/determined foot of at least average quality; +1 general fighting
  within range/4; +1 standard wagon adjacent.
- Rally: chance each turn to test up one level (always with a general). No rally if dropped last
  turn, pursued, or shot >10% last enemy turn. Routers dispersed after 3 rout moves.
- Autobreak at ~50% losses (average; better troops later, raw sooner).
- Rout spreads: units adjacent to a breaking unit test (not for broken lights unless they're lights).
- Pursuit: mounted (and elephants) pursue at least once; warbands usually; raw foot sometimes; other
  foot never. Pursuers can't be ordered; they hit fresh enemy met in their path.

## Terrain (ch. 16)
- 4 levels: **Difficult** (woods, marsh, difficult slopes): lights fine; medium foot/warriors/bowmen/
  mob disordered; **everyone else severely disordered**. **Rough**: lights, medium foot, warriors,
  bowmen, mob fine; **heavily/fully armoured knights and cataphracts severely disordered**; others
  disordered. **Non-open** (enclosures, some streams): no disorder but combat isn't "in open terrain"
  (kills lancer bonus). **Open**: no effect.
- Disorder ends as soon as the unit moves onto non-rough, non-difficult ground.
- Fighting into terrain: the attacker outside suffers the defender's terrain disorder; attacking out
  of terrain doesn't. Never "open" either way.
- **Custom battle setup (5.5-5.7):** Map Size = battlefield width (raised automatically for bigger
  forces unless set in Advanced). Map Type = region (North European, N.E. Frozen, Mediterranean,
  Middle-Eastern) x terrain (Agricultural, Hilly, Wooded, Mountains, Marshy, Plains; also Steppe,
  Desert, Tropical) - it sets the random map generator's parameters; even mountain regions can have
  open valleys. Plains = no hills or marshes, fewer woods/rough. "Many Agricultural maps are also
  mostly open terrain." Pot Luck = a region fitting one side, terrain heavily weighted to Agricultural.
  Choosing your own force (Player) in single player gives a slightly smaller force (5.7).
  The manual does not list what an Agricultural map contains -> photo-map + tooltips will show.
- **Some terrain is INVISIBLE (24.1, 24.3):** Enclosure, Medium Stream, Deep Stream and Dead Ground are
  transparent overlays that change a square's terrain type without changing its look, and an invisible
  Rough overlay can make an open-looking slope Rough. So a photo shows woods/marsh/hills/buildings/water
  but NOT every non-open or rough square -> for squares that matter (where the line stands, where knights
  will charge) read the square's tooltip (terrain type + height; no height shown = 0).
- Streams by size (16.1, 24.3): small = open/non-open, "large" = rough going, "deep" = difficult going.
- Movement cost: open 4 AP straight / 6 diagonal; "other terrain types may have higher movement costs for
  some or all troop-types" (11.1) - the manual gives no numbers -> read it off the game's move highlights.
- Visibility (16.4): woods, buildings and marsh conceal STATIONARY foot inside them. Woods, built-up
  areas and higher ground block line of sight (small single buildings may not). Squares I can't see are
  darkened; yellow "?" marks squares at the edge of concealing terrain that may hide enemy (option).
- Water impassable (except bridges/fords); mountains, cliffs impassable. Built-up areas protect foot
  from flank/rear charges. Higher ground: +25 POA (<=75 height diff), +100 (>=100).
- Obstacles (16.6, on tile edges - irrigation ditches, low walls, hedges, fortifications): foot in a
  square with an obstacle on an edge benefit against enemy beyond the line of that edge; they count as
  Protected vs mounted and vs flank/rear charges across it; hedges/walls/ditches cover stationary foot on
  either side - cover lost if they move, even to turn (not for fortifications). Fighting across any
  obstacle is never "in open terrain" (kills lancer bonus). Ditches: protection, +100 POA pure missile foot vs mounted, +25
  others. Field fortifications: 33% cover, +100 POA foot. Heavy fortifications: 75% cover, +200, and
  difficult for attackers. Charging out across an obstacle loses its benefits. Stakes: like ditches,
  and heavily armoured foot attacking them count as in rough (disordered).

## Victory (ch. 18)
- An army breaks at **60% routed/dispersed**, or **40-59% if the enemy has lost at least 25% less**.
  Counted by units (size-weighted), not casualties. Off-map evaders count half.
- **Time limit in custom/open battles: the AI wins a timed-out game even if it has lost more.** I must
  actually break the enemy before the last turn - don't dawdle.
- Standard wagon: counts as 4 heavy foot units for rout %, +1 cohesion to adjacent friends; keep it
  near the centre facing the enemy.

## Hints (ch. 23) and the AI (ch. 31)
- Keep units in command range. Cohesion matters more than casualties. Concentrate shooting on one
  unit; get troops in position before the enemy is in range. Best matchups; never charge frontally
  into enemy who win the matchup. Gang up. Set up flank charges after a frontal engagement. Foot shock
  follow-ups can expose flanks.
- AI: same rules, no hidden bonuses; difficulty = force balance. Outmatched infantry armies don't
  advance; their cavalry hangs back too; lights stay close unless they overmatch. Units pick a target
  each turn, avoid disordering terrain (heavies), avoid flank exposure, seek flanks and wait for a
  frontal attack first, avoid bad matchups, evade when outmatched, shooters stay out of enemy range,
  near-broken lights retreat.

## Hidden units (the user, 2026-11-02)
In FoG II units are generally only hidden in FORESTS. Open ground, fields, hills: everything shows. So a
"scout first" turn is only worth it when woods near the enemy could hide something - otherwise photograph
the board, count both armies and start the plan (battle 2: the whole French army was visible at turn 2).

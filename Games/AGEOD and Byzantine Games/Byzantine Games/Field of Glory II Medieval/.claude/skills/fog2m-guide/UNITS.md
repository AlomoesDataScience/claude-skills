## Troop types (29.2) and their rule effects
| Type | What it is | Rules that follow from it |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy Foot | close-formation foot (most spearmen) | resilient; +1 cohesion if at least average (not close-order warbands = undrilled heavy impact foot) (p.74); disordered by difficult (severely), rough (disordered); undrilled = unmanoeuvrable |
| Determined Foot | fast resolute heavy foot (Swiss) | as heavy foot |
| Medium Foot | lighter close-order foot (Welsh, Kerns, Brigans) | less hurt by terrain: rough = no disorder, difficult = disordered; -1 cohesion losing to mounted/heavy foot in open |
| Warriors | tribal warbands (Galwegians) | as medium foot for terrain and the -1 |
| Bowmen | massed bow/crossbow/longbow foot | very vulnerable to mounted in open; -1 as medium foot; if no melee ability: -70 shooting with enemy combat troops within 2 sq facing them, -1 cohesion losing any combat |
| Light Foot | dispersed skirmishers | not disordered by terrain; evade non-light chargers (unless in rough/difficult/behind obstacle); can't charge unbroken non-lights (except in rough/difficult); pass through friends front-back; free turn any direction; shooting at them -100; non-lights ignore their ZOC |
| Mob | untrained rabble (Peasants) | untrained = re-roll 6s on cohesion; unmanoeuvrable; -1 as medium foot |
| Knights | European knights on heavy horses | unmanoeuvrable (11.4.2); Heavily/Fully Armoured knights severely disordered by rough; all non-lights except medium foot/warriors/bowmen/mob severely disordered by difficult (16.1) |
| Cavalry | other non-light horse | most can turn 90 deg (8 AP) and still move 1-2 squares (11.4.3); non-lancer cavalry may evade (11.13.4); rough = disordered, difficult = severely disordered (16.1) |
| Light Horse | skirmishing horse | as light troops (evade, free turn); Small [card] |

## Card lines
- Size: Small (lights) [card], Average, Large (10+ models: unmanoeuvrable, big artillery target, heavy-weapon
  ones get +15 Overlappers in melee), Very Large (mobs) [card]. Combat strength counts 2 ranks of foot / 4 horse.
- Quality: Raw/Untrained (re-roll 6s; unmanoeuvrable; autobreak sooner; raw foot may pursue),
  Average, Superior (re-roll 1s), Elite (1s and 2s); -50..+100 POA in every combat.
- Armour: Unprotected < Lightly Protected [card; position = hypothesis] < Protected < Armoured < Heavily Armoured < Fully Armoured.
  Melee "better armour" up to +100 vs foot / +50 vs mounted (enemy heavy weapon cancels 50). Shooting:
  sliding scale - bows/javelins -75 vs Protected, -150 Armoured, -225 Heavily; longbow -125/-175;
  crossbow -125/-155 (-165 vs Fully).
- Special features: Unmanoeuvrable (no free 45 turn); Can form square (pike/deep spear: immune to
  flank/rear, no ZOC, no charge, 1 square per turn); Large artillery target.
- (D) = may dismount (deployment phase only).

## Capabilities (29.3) - phase and POA (13.2 tables, checked line by line against PDF p.69-70 on 2026-11-02)
| Capability | Phase | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Bow / Crossbow / Longbow | shooting | range 2/4 (foot); crossbow & longbow pierce armour best |
| Javelins | shooting | range 1 |
| Impact Foot | impact | +200 vs foot (+10 more if 3+ ranks), +100 vs mounted unless they charge mounted shock troops |
| Light Spear | impact | foot +100 (not when charging mounted shock); mounted +50 except vs knightly lancers |
| Defensive Spearmen | impact+melee | if not Fragmented or Severely Disordered: impact +100 vs any if not charging, or vs defensive spearmen; melee +100 vs any. Steady non-charging ones cut lancers to +150 / other lancers to +50 |
| Offensive Spearmen | impact+melee | if not Fragmented or Severely Disordered: impact +100 vs any unless charging mounted shock troops, +50 more vs knightly lancers if non-charging deep (started 3 models deep); melee +100 vs any. Steady non-charging ones cut lancer bonus as above |
| Pike | impact+melee | if not Fragmented or Severely Disordered: +100 vs any (impact: except mounted shock troops the pikes are charging); if not Disrupted or Disordered: +100 more if 4 ranks deep (impact: only in open terrain; melee: any) |
| Swordsmen (foot) | melee | +100 vs mounted; +100 vs foot, only +50 vs foot that are steady offensive/defensive spearmen or defending an obstacle |
| Swordsmen (mounted) | melee | knights +150 vs any mounted; other mounted swordsmen +100 vs any mounted; +100 vs foot unless these are steady pike/offensive/defensive spearmen or defending an obstacle |
| Heavy Weapon | impact+melee | +100 (impact: not when charging mounted shock); +10 if armoured; cancels 50 of enemy armour |
| Knightly Lancers | impact, OPEN only | +200 vs foot (+150 vs steady non-charging spear/pike); +100 vs mounted |
| Other Lancers | impact, OPEN only | +100 vs mounted except knightly lancers; +100 vs foot (+50 vs steady spear/pike) |
| Any mounted | impact | +100 vs light foot, bowmen, mob |
Partial capability (13.2.9): at IMPACT, units with >=33% Light Spear, Heavy Weapon or Defensive Spearmen, or >=50% Lancers, get the full POA; in MELEE, Swordsmen, Heavy Weapon and Defensive Spearmen get their actual % of the POA.
"Pure missile troops" behind ditches/stakes: +100 vs mounted.

# PARANOIA - starter guide

Status 2026-10-29: core book (Mongoose, published 2023) outline and example of play read. Not
played. Purchased book: keep local.

Files: `The_Paranoia_Core_Book.pdf` (146 pages) and `.txt`.

## What it is (Claude's summary)

Players are Troubleshooters, clones in the underground city of Alpha Complex, sent by the
all-seeing, insane Computer to root out traitors - while each of them secretly belongs to a
banned secret society and hides a mutant power, both treason. Death is cheap (each player has
backup clones); the fun is in accusing each other, bluffing The Computer and surviving its
contradictory orders. The GM often speaks *as* The Computer.

Mechanics (rules chapter, p12+): roll a dice pool built from a stat plus a skill (e.g.
Mechanics + Science) plus modifiers such as an item's level (the example adds it to NODE); The
Computer's suspicion is tracked as **Treason Stars** - collect enough and you are terminated.
Wounds go from Hurt to worse. Missions come from a briefing, involve R&D's experimental (and
dangerous) gadgets, and end in a debriefing where blame is handed out.

## How Claude helps

Claude can play The Computer (cheerful, paranoid, contradictory), run a mission from chapter 4,
keep each player's secret society goals and mutant powers private, and track Treason Stars and
clones. Keep it funny; summarise the book in your own words.

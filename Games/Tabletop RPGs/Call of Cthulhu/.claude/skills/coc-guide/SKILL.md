## What it is (Claude's summary)

Ordinary people (investigators: professors, journalists, detectives) uncover horrors from beyond,
classically in 1920s New England. Knowledge costs sanity; violence rarely solves anything; the
win is usually surviving and stopping the cult or the summoning, at a price.

Core mechanics (Keeper Rulebook ch. 5 game system):
- **d100 roll-under** the skill or characteristic: a regular success is at or under the value,
  hard is half, extreme a fifth; 01 critical, 100 (or 96+ for low skills) a fumble. The Keeper
  sets the difficulty (regular, hard, extreme) instead of modifiers.
- **Bonus and penalty dice**: roll extra tens dice and keep the best or worst.
- **Pushing a roll**: retry a failed roll once with a justified new effort; failing a pushed roll
  brings something worse.
- **Luck** can be spent to adjust rolls (optional rule); skill improvement checks after sessions.
- **Sanity** (ch. 8): seeing the Mythos costs SAN points; big losses bring temporary or indefinite
  insanity and bouts of madness; Cthulhu Mythos skill rises as sanity falls.
- **Combat** (ch. 6) is fast and deadly; **chases** (ch. 7) have their own system.

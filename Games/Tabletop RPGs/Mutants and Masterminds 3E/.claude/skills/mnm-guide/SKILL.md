# Mutants & Masterminds 3E - starter guide

Status 2026-10-29: both Deluxe books here, outlines read. Not played. Purchased books: keep local.

Files: `Mutants and Masterminds Deluxe Heroes Handbook 3E.pdf`, `Deluxe Gamemasters Guide 3E.pdf`,
each with a `.txt`; `MNM 3E.zip` is the original download.

## What it is (Claude's summary)

Superheroes built with power points: everything (abilities, skills, advantages, powers) is
bought from one budget set by the **power level (PL)**, which also caps attack and defence
bonuses so heroes stay comparable. Rolls are **d20 + modifier vs a difficulty**. Powers are
**effects** (Damage, Affliction, Flight, Protection, Senses...) shaped by extras and flaws, so any
comic power can be modelled. Damage is not hit points: the target makes a **Toughness resistance
check** and suffers worsening conditions (bruised, dazed, staggered, incapacitated). **Hero
points** let players re-roll or edit the scene; the GM gives them for complications.

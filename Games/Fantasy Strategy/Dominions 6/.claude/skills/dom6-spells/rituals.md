# Rituals that are neither combat spells nor summons, ranked for Pythium (first pass, 2026-10-28, for the user's review)

These are the rituals cast on the world rather than in battle: scrying, remote kills, travel,
gem logistics, site searching, economy globals, province curses, wards and walls. The user
asked to consider them ("we should consider non-combat, non-summon rituals").

There are 184 researchable non-summon rituals; 95 are on paths we have. They were
extracted from the inspector (ritual spells whose effect isn't a summon) and ranked by hand
against the plan:
- win big battles;
- kill mage corps without losing anything important;
- grow research and gems.

**Who casts:** ordinary rituals use the caster's own skill only; communion doesn't help them.
- **Th** = Theurg (A1 W1 S2);
- **AT** = Arch Theurg (A2 W1 S3 + a random +1);
- **AT+S / AT+A** = an Astral- or Air-rolled Arch Theurg, or one with a booster;
- **Barth** = Bartholomeus (A2 W2 S3);
- **God** = Hortus once awake (E5 N5);
- **GC** = Grand Communion. It works for globals only and sums the Acolytes', Theurgs' and Arch Theurgs' skills.

Pythium's gem income is mostly astral (S5 a month at the start), so cheap astral rituals cost
us little.

## What it changes for research

- **Thaumaturgy moves up.** Thaumaturgy 3-4 brings:
  - Teleport, the gem and item transport, Auspex and Astral Window;
  - the god's site searches.
  Thaumaturgy 2 (Mind Burn) and 5 (Soul Slay) are what Mind Hunt attacks with.
- **Enchantment 5 moves up** for Ritual of Returning; Dispel, Eyes of God and Spell Ward come
  with it.
- **Alteration 5** has the god's Mother Oak as well as Maws of the Earth.
- **Evocation 6** pairs Mind Hunt with the Wrath of God.
- **Now, with no research:** Astral Projection (Evocation 1) is already ours. A Theurg can
  scry an attack target for 2 astral pearls.

## Globals: casting, protecting and losing them (illwiki `globals` page, Dom6)

- **Slots:** 5 by default. Casting when they're full attacks a random existing global. Casting a
  global that's already up attacks that copy.
- **Overcast** (protection against being dispelled):
  - +1 per gem spent over the minimum (up to 100 × caster's path level);
  - +5 per path level over the requirement;
  - **Grand Communion adds +1 per path level of every Grand Communicant helping**, in the same
    province, the same turn (it works for globals and dispels only).
  - Each side rolls overcast + an open-ended d20; the higher one wins.
  - **For Pythium:** our Grand Communion of Theurgs and Acolytes is also our dispel
    protection. Cast a global from the capital with every Acolyte helping.
- **Ways a global ends:**
  - Dispel (only overcast vs overcast), Disenchantment (halves overcast even when it fails);
  - Elemental Oppositions (Evocation 7, path 5, 60 gems: every global of one element);
  - Arcane Disruption (Thaumaturgy 9: all globals);
  - **the caster dies** (resurrection doesn't save it; the caster has a green pentagram);
  - **the caster is feebleminded** (Mind Hunt, Nether Darts...);
  - province-tied globals end when the province is taken.
- **So the caster of our globals must be safe:**
  - keep him in the capital under a Dome of Arcane Warding (protects against Mind Hunt);
  - don't make the god the caster if he'll be fighting.

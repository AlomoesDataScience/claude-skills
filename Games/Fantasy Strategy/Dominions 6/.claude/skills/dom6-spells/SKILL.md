# Dom6 spells

A growing knowledge base of combat spells, built one category at a time at the user's request
(2026-10-28): "building a complete knowledge of all the combat spells in the game piece by piece".

Done:
- buffs and debuffs, ranked for melee and missile troops;
- the list of what Pythium can cast;
- every Evocation spell, ranked against troop blocks and mage lines (`evocations.md`);
- every summoning spell of every school, ranked by what its summons kill (`summons.md`);
- every killing spell of every school, ranked against blocks and mage lines (`kill-spells.md`);
- the non-combat, non-summon rituals, ranked for Pythium (`rituals.md`).

Next pieces: battlefield enchantments and clouds in depth; the non-summon Conjuration spells (powers, Tangle Vines, Maggots).

The user reviews each page; their corrections go into the page and into dom6-knowledge.

## Files

- `scripts/spells.py`:
  - `list`: every combat spell as a table, with filters `--paths A2W2S3H4`, `--kind buff|debuff|damage|battlefield|summon|other`, `--school`, `--max-level`, `--name`, `--all` (also innate and national spells).
  - `castable`: rewrites `castable.md`.
  - `melee X Y --x BUFFS --y BUFFS`: rounds to kill, both ways.
  - `missile SHOOTER TARGET`: damage per 100 shots into a packed block.
  - `rank`: the standard battery behind the ranking page; takes about a minute.
  - `damage [--school Evocation] [--spells A,B] [--range 25] [-n 300]`: per-cast kills and damage of attack spells against packed enemy blocks and a mage line, at the minimum path level and +2; it reads every effect block of a spell (secondary splashes, chains). It writes nothing; `evocations-table.md` is its saved output.
  - `summons [-n 2500]`: every summoning spell (all schools) ranked by enemy heavy infantry and militia its summons kill in melee before dying (trample, awe, invulnerability, ethereal, resistances, fatigue cap), total and per gem; `summons-table.md` is its saved output.
  - `damage --school ""` runs the damage model on every school; `kill-table.md` keeps the killing spells from it.
  - The buff tokens are listed in the script's docstring.
- `castable.md`: the 258 combat spells some Pythium caster can cast, by kind. Each row shows the cheapest caster (Theurg, Arch Theurg, Bartholomeus, communion levels, the god), its research level, and ✓ if castable now. Regenerate after research or when new casters arrive; update the `researched` dict and `CASTERS` in the script first.
- `evocations.md`: all 94 Evocation spells ranked S to D against troop blocks and mage corps, with corrections (Arcane Bolt, Solar Rays and Cleansing Water don't hurt men; Astral Geyser and Freezing Mist are weak) and Pythium's Evocation path. `evocations-table.md` holds the numbers.
- `summons.md` + `summons-table.md`: battle summons and permanent summons in tiers, Pythium's summon path (Lesser Air Elemental, Gryphons, Angel of the Host, Draconians, Living Clouds; the god's Spirits of the Wood and Iron Pigs).
- `kill-spells.md` + `kill-table.md`: killing spells of all schools in tiers, mage killers, target-limited spells (Maws of the Earth, Alteration 5, is the awake god's area nuke).
- `rituals.md`: the non-combat, non-summon rituals (scrying, remote kills, travel, gem transport, site searches, economy globals, wards) ranked for Pythium, and what they change in research; plus how globals are protected and lost (overcast, Grand Communion, caster death).
- `community-notes.md`: what the illwiki guides say about combat magic (early power spells, the buffball, midgame threats, fear enchantments) to weigh against our measured rankings. The casting AI's rules are in dom6-tactics `ai-casting.md`; the full wiki is the dom6-wiki skill.
- `buffs-debuffs.md`: the ranked families (S to D) for melee troops, missile troops, debuffs, mage protection and mage killing, with the calculator tables and why. Ends with what it means for Pythium's research and the open questions to test in battle.
- Builds on **dom6-inspector** (spell, unit and weapon data), **dom6-mechanics** (combat rules), **dom6-tactics** (enemies met, battle log) and **dom6-army-check** (where scripts get set).

Run Python with `python3 -I` (system Python with PIL only; no pip).

## Workflow

1. A question about one spell: `spells.py list --name REGEX`, then the inspector page for the full text.
2. Is a buff worth it? `spells.py melee OURS THEIRS --x buff` or `--y debuff`, then compare with `none`. Multiply by coverage per cast (area in squares × 3 men) and check that the range reaches from the caster's position.
3. After research finishes or before a big battle: read `castable.md` (✓ column) and the ranking, then rescript with dom6-army-check.
4. Record new lessons from battles (what the AI targeted, real hit rates) on the ranking page under Open questions.

## Facts and gotchas

- Inspector spell pages: line 2 is the research ("Evocation 2", "Divine", "unresearchable"); line 4 is "Combat" or "Ritual". **National spells have a nation line before it**; MA Pythium (nation 56) has no national combat spells. Records' `nations` field lists the allowed nations.
- A spell page holds **secondary effect blocks** after the main one, each with its own `spell id`, damage and area. Parse per block; the main block's values come first.
- Flags that limit targets: `magiconly` (magic beings only: Arcane Bolt, Hidden Flame), `demonundead` (Solar Rays, Cleansing Water), `uwonly`, `sizeresist` (knockdown), `mind`.
- Damage codes: 1000+N = N + caster path level ("+"); 2000+N = N + 2 per level ("++").
- **Area column:** `1` means one battlefield square (10 size points, about 3 humans), as on Barkskin and Blur. `One person` means a single unit. `Caster` means self only. (The user caught a misreading, 2026-10-28.)
- Underwater-only spells (`uwonly`: Gooey Water, Water Strike, Encase in Ice, Ice Shield...) look great but **can't be cast on land**.
- Natural protection and armour combine as armour + natural − armour × natural / 40 (checked against inspector totals). Skins add natural protection up to a cap (Barkskin 10, Stoneskin 15, Ironskin 20).
- Unit pages without a "(basic, head, body)" breakdown give one protection value: treat it as natural protection everywhere (fixed in `load_unit`).
- Summons are judged in melee only: missile summons, commanders' magic, morale (undisciplined animals rout) and magic weapons against ethereal units need judging by hand.
- **Shields** (fixed 2026-10-28): weapon rows are `#id name | dmg | att | len`, shield rows have five fields; the armour slot comes from the inspector's armour page (`armor_slot`). Missiles and blockable spells use the manual's roll: DRN + size points/2 (+2 magic) vs DRN + 2 × parry, a loss misses. Spells flagged `ignoreshield` (noShield) skip it. Pages written before the fix carry a correction note.
- The model's damage per hit came out 1-2× higher than the Murias battle review (that was before the shield fix; recheck in the next battle). Use it for comparisons only.
- Items are ranked in the **dom6-items** skill, which reuses this model.
- Earth- and Nature-path spells in `castable.md` are the god's (E5 N5), who is dormant until about turns 10-13.

# Mounts (Dominions 6, vanilla)

The user's list (2026-11-03). Manual pp.64-65; illwiki `mounted`, `skilled-rider`. Centaurs are NOT mounted units
(one body) - they still get lance charges (wiki).

## Two units in one (p.64)
Rider and mount are targeted and wounded separately; one can die and the other stay. A riderless horse
routs; some monsters (gryphons) keep fighting.

## Who gets hit (p.64; wiki)
1. Mount 3 / 4 / 5+ sizes bigger than the rider: 25 / 50 / 75% the mount is hit first.
2. Otherwise: missiles 50/50; melee: mount if its size >= reach + 2, else 30% + 10% x mount size - 10% x reach
   (min 10%). Reach = attacker size + weapon length: long weapons and big attackers hit the rider.
- AoE (except trample, Earthquake) and lightning/shock hit BOTH. **Trample always hits the mount.**
- Several riders: one at random.

## Combat stats (wiki)
- Rider uses the mount's combat speed and map move. Rider armour encumbrance counts HALF in melee (not for
  spellcasting); the mount carries 3 + rider size - mount size encumbrance (min 0).
- Rider +3 defence if targeted (old rule). Two-handed weapon: -3 attack (p.64).
- **Skilled Rider**: mount gets morale and defence; the defence bonus shrinks by the barding's encumbrance.
- Lances: charge bonus on the first hit each battle (dom6-weapons).

## Morale and falling (p.64-65)
- Fear effects (fear auras, Terror) hit both. Rider routs = both leave. Mount breaks alone = rider thrown, mount
  flees, rider fights on. Squad/army rout checks use only the rider.
- Falling: open-ended d(mount size - rider size), armour-negating. Chariots: no fall damage.
- Rider killed: mount loses Skilled Rider bonus, makes a harder individual morale check (wiki: ~10).

## Items (p.64)
One magic saddle or barding on a commander's mount; some rider items also cover the mount (item text says).

## Losing the mount (p.65; wiki)
- Riders with no spare mount walk home (uncontrolled, morale checks each turn - fail = lost); at home they can be
  recruited again at HALF cost with their XP and afflictions (blue number on the recruit screen).
- Riderless "Smart Mount" units also go home (chance per turn). A dismounted rider and a matching free mount
  in one province rejoin.
- Mounted COMMANDERS stay: take a regular unit's mount, or order "Reclaim Mount" (a month).

## Using it
- Against cavalry: long weapons (reach hits riders), mass missiles (half land on horses: horses rout riders out),
  fear and morale attacks (thrown riders), trample.
- Our side: a mounted commander is safer from short weapons; Falco (Tir na n'Og) rides.
- Pangaea's White Centaurs are not mounts: one unit, lance charge, sacred, Blur-protected (dom6-scbm notes).

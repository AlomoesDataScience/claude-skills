# Supercombatants and cheese (Dominions 6)

The user's ask (2026-11-03): "skills to create and defeat different forms of supercombatants... army wipe thug
that relies on cheese". Main source: LucidTactics, "Dominions 6 - Guide To Tanking & Fine Cheeses" (video
8-Cq8Ktw-sc, transcript via video-watch; timestamps [mm:ss]). Order of truth: game > manual > video > wiki -
rules below are checked against the manual or inspector where noted. Thugs (cheaper, army or raiding):
dom6-thugs. Mods (SCBM) change items and spells: check dom6-scbm first.

## The idea: hard counters everywhere [00:28-04:47, 57:26]
- Most units have a hard counter, and the counters go round in a circle. In Lucid's demo [07:36-11:45],
  elite humans lose to Anakites (regeneration + blood bond + fortitude); the Anakites lose to Vanheim
  sacreds with high defence and unholy weapons; those lose to a protection-tank golem; the golem loses to
  Vanjarls with Greatswords of Sharpness; and the Vanjarls lose to a crowd of Anakites.
- "There's always a way to kill whatever kind of monster you come up with" [57:26] - an SC is a bet that the
  enemy won't bring the counter in time.
- Against new players, be the problem others must solve (sledgehammer); among experts, not getting cheesed
  is worth more (Swiss army knife) [02:40].

## 1. Protection tanks [05:00-19:55]
- **What**: protection so high that hits roll 0 damage. Protection = N + A - N x A / 40 (wiki; matches the
  game: armour 22 + natural 20 = 31 at [13:51]). Video rule of thumb: the higher value plus half the lower.
- **Build**: a high-natural chassis (golem; Iron Skin sets natural protection), Armor of the Knights, Temper
  Armor, bracers, a brand for area kills, Quickness. Spells: Summon Earth Power, Personal Iron Skin, Temper
  Armor, Blessing [13:19].
- **Weak to**: armour-piercing (halves protection: Greatsword of Sharpness, Hunter's Knife, Kris, Knife of
  the Damned, Blade of Grass, Weapons of Sharpness / Steel Slice), armour-negating (Dusk Dagger, Drain Life,
  Lightning Bolt), **trample** (armour-piercing), big water elementals, **fatigue** (encumbrance >
  reinvigoration -> fatigue -> critical hits that ignore armour: dom6-armor) [15:53-19:36].

## 2. Defence tanks [19:55-37:38]
- **What**: nothing hits. Harassment is the enemy: every directed melee attack gives -1 defence, fading by a
  percentage (manual "Harassed"; multi-attack weapons harass per attack; ranged and area don't).
  Surrounded by three humans a square, defence fell from the high 20s to 18 [21:03].
- **Build**: Mistform (blows do little; ends on a very hard hit or a magic weapon - inspector), **Shroud of
  Bewilderment** (attackers confused - the video: an MR check before the attack roll, so no harassment either;
  spirit sight sees through it), Displaced Body (glamour), Vine Shield (entangles two attackers a round, MR),
  parrying weapon or Frost Brand, Air Shield vs archers; reached 31 defence with 3 harassment [23:44].
- Big hitters with few attacks a square (giants) are WORSE against it than many small attackers [24:46].
- **Weak to**: many attacks (multi-attack weapons, Quickness, horned helmet, stone birds, Burning Pearl
  attack), chaff and swarms (Lifelong Protection imps, skeleton spam), MR buffs on the attackers (Shroud and
  Vine Shield are MR checks - Antimagic for the army), area and evocation spells (no attack roll), outlasting
  it to round 100 when buffs end [30:16].
- In 1v1 fights defence beats protection: an AP weapon is useless if it never hits [31:50].

## 3. Fatigue tanks [37:38-49:28]
- **What**: Phoenix Pyre - dies, explodes (10 AP fire, area 50), comes back elsewhere; each death costs
  30 + d20 fatigue and it fails while unconscious (inspector). Stack reinvigoration (armour, boots, amulet),
  berserk (Flesh Eater) for morale, Soul Vortex, Fire Shield.
- Hard to make work: quick killers reach each new spot and kill it again; battlefield enchantments and Pyre end
  at **round 100** (manual p.71) [47:44].
- **Weak to**: fast killers (Haste, mass flight, Wind Runner), fatigue weapons (Axe of Hate, Star of Darkness),
  draw blood (Dusk Dagger) [48:16].

## 4. HP tanks [49:28-53:15]
- **What**: so much HP and regeneration that damage never catches up - Irminsul with regeneration bless, ring
  of regeneration, Body Ethereal, Astral Shield, Personal Regeneration [50:29]; "hit for 30 again and again,
  still at 250".
- **Weak to**: pure burst damage on a melee hero, or a different attack vector (below).

## 5. Magic attack vectors [53:15-58:24]
- Protection and resistances are both subtractive (resistance also gives a % cut after) [53:16].
- Spells skip attack/defence: Pillar of Fire, Banefire darts from two cheap fire mages on Advance and cast
  behind 25 PD killed a ~30-gem elf tank [54:50-56:24] - "not a very good trade".
- Soul Slay (MR or die; mindless are immune), armour-negating elemental (Lightning Bolt, Incinerate), most fire
  is armour-piercing. Defend with MR and the right elemental resistances (dom6-magic-resistance).

## Protecting an army from cheese [58:25-1:02:33]
- Big blocks of cheap troops are juicy cheese targets; killy sacreds with high attack or unholy weapons are hard
  to cheese, not impossible.
- Send mixes that beat the likely occupants (vs elves: answers to defence thugs). Carry countermeasures: mages
  with gems on Advance and cast, a counter-thug with an AP weapon, MR buffs, armour-piercing troops, flyers to
  interrupt scripts.
- **Wading**: a unit 3+ sizes bigger can move through smaller units' squares - big killers reach the hard
  target past screening chaff [1:01:00] (video; not found in the manual text - test before relying on it).
- If your god alone can wipe their army, send only the god [1:02:02].
- Items are situational: the all-purpose AP two-hander is good most of the time, but Swords of Swiftness win
  against a defence thug [1:04:09]. Against the AI: its summoned drakes and cubes punish lone thugs [1:05:14].

## Darkwolf's view (9oUdOWyzkSw, in dom6-thugs)
An SC is tailored to what it will meet; teleport in before the target prepares; finish before twilight (round 100);
keep an escape (returning, a stealth item) so it isn't landed on; returning procs at the end of a phase.

## Independents: how hard, and how to beat them

Difficulty is the guide's own rating (out of 10). **Check the map sprite**: the same name
covers very different troops (14, 16 or 18-protection heavy cavalry).

| Independents | Difficulty | What kills our troops | How to beat them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Militia | 1 (2 shielded) | nothing | anything works; their rout spreads to the rest of the province |
| Bear tribe | 1 | numbers | they split into a javelin group and a melee group: take them in halves |
| Archers | 1.5 | arrows on unarmoured, unshielded troops | armour and shields nullify them; put a shielded squad where it draws the fire |
| Deer tribe | 2 | every unit shoots | easy; just armour up |
| Ghouls, soulless | 2 | surrounding small parties | holy priests with Banishment (H3 is best), cheap infantry; don't send a tiny party |
| Light infantry | 2.5 | not much | faster than other foot, so they arrive first |
| Light cavalry | 2.5 | melee when out of ammo | fine unless the party is razor-thin |
| Wolf tribe | 2-5 | two daggers each: harassment drops our defence; flanks collapse fast | enough width not to be surrounded; kill the melee first; they rout fast in our dominion |
| Jaguar tribe | 3 | all slingers (blunt): hurts light infantry on the way in | helmets, shields, armour, numbers; commanders at the back can be sniped |
| Lion tribe | 3.5 | big shields, spears and javelins: the best tribe against arrows | heavy infantry or cavalry; light foot routs to javelins; the commander and mage can be sniped |
| Horse tribe | 3.5 | many horse archers; deadly if they run out of arrows and close in | reach critical mass; chaff dies to them |
| Crossbows | 3-8 | high armour-piercing shots: deadly to slow heavy armour stuck behind a blocker | collapse the front fast, attack-rear cavalry, a big-shield bait squad, or skirmish-formation archers |
| Heavy infantry (12-16 prot) | 6.5 | hit hard, shrug off weak troops; the count decides everything | more good troops than they have; rout the others around them; archers do little |
| Lizardfolk | 7 | barbarian damage with long weapons and better morale; kill pretenders | javelins and arrows; a big party that clears them at impact; they're weaker in cold provinces |
| Barbarians | 8 | many high-damage hits; kill pretenders; commanders "unsnipable" | **a line as wide as their blob** (spears, repel), **lots of archers** (even bad ones), Evocation 1-2 spells, or a morale shock. Don't send a thin line of high-protection short-sword elites |
| Bone tribe | 8.5 | berserk, high morale, reach weapons, mages; punch through armour | archers, then high damage; evocations; armour is mostly wasted |
| Heavy cavalry | 8.5 | lance charge hits first | lance catchers (chaff in front), deploy in a corner on hold and attack, then surround; 10 vs 20 is a different fight: **ping first** |
| Jade Maidens | 8.5 | many high-defence cavalry plus mages | lots of good bodies; chaff dies |
| Vine men + Bloodhenge Druids | 10 | the druids summon ~50 imps | a big force and a plan for the mages; avoid early |
| Elephants | high | rout dice, numbers | |

**Barbarians, bone tribe, lizardfolk and heavy cavalry are the ones that kill expander gods.**

## Party size and speed

- Expansion has two phases:
  - **resource-capped** before the capital circle is taken;
  - **gold-capped** after it.
  Small parties suit the capital circle; medium-large parties suit the rest.
- **Razor-edge parties** need a damage source plus a frontline of low-value blockers. Rough
  minimums from the guide:
  - 3 heavy cavalry hitting the commanders is fairly reliable, 2 is dicey;
  - a mixed line should be wider than the independents' front and at least ¼ medium infantry.
- **Ping before a risky attack:** send a cheap commander on Retreat. The replay shows the exact
  army and formation, and the commander often comes back.
  - Scout reports can be far off (ours: Helmshire said 30 and had 44).
  - Astral Projection does the same job from range.
- **Mercenaries** cost ⅕-½ of the raw unit price and are a ready-made party; good bands at
  400-600 gold pay back quickly.
- **Playing to morale** (rout them without being able to beat them) gives small parties but
  can wipe a party on bad dice. The guide tells new players not to.

## Frontage, formations and timing

- **Frontage wins:** more of our men in contact, fewer surrounded (each extra attacker lowers
  defence).
  - **Line** is usually the best formation.
  - **Two small boxes with a gap** make a fake line; units from the back of a big box take a
    while to backfill.
  - **Box** is a big arrow target.
  - **Skirmish** is for our archers against many archers (it carries a morale penalty).
  - **Loose line** spreads archers.
  - **Double line** is rarely useful.
- **Impact timing:**
  - same-type units should hit together;
  - high-damage, low-defence units should hit a moment later, so the blockers take the first
    blows;
  - cavalry going round the back should hit after the lines engage.
  - **Never let berserkers or light cavalry run ahead alone.**
- **Javelins** stop the line from forming, so they cost frontage. Throw them at unshielded,
  unarmoured enemies. Against shields, the author prefers attacking for the better line.
- **Hold and Attack:**
  - **Use it against heavy cavalry** (they charge ahead and get surrounded alone) and against
    fliers (it keeps the commanders covered).
  - **Avoid it against many archers** (we eat extra volleys).
  - Against infantry it funnels them in together. **Plain Attack** picks apart a divided enemy.
- **Attack Rear is unreliable:** units get caught by interceptors. Use moderately strong
  cavalry, all on one flank, to kill commanders or archers.
- **Archers:**
  - **Fire Closest** is the default: it routs the infantry, then the archers.
  - Fire at Archers trades archers and splits our damage.
  - Fire at Large Monsters is anti-cavalry.

## Magic in expansion (the guide's tests)

- **Evocation 1 + Alteration 2 beats rushing Alteration 3 for expansion.**
  - Star Fires: 5 armour-negating to 3 enemies, at a 75% cast speed. It finishes off chip
    damage.
  - Gift of Cheated Fate is nearly free under Hold and Attack and protects the units that would
    otherwise die.
  - Body Ethereal elites in small parties tend to lose their commander.
- Good expansion spells:
  - Alteration: self buffs, Blur, Gift of Cheated Fate.
  - Evocation: Fire Darts, Flame Bolt, Geyser, Star Fires, Bewitching Lights, Flare, Web,
    Warrior Illusion.
  - Conjuration: none.
- **A mage that lets a party take one extra province pays for itself in 1-2 turns.**
- **Gems can save fatigue** if the mage is the only answer to armour.

## For Pythium (our game)

- **Pythium's strengths for expansion:**
  - Serpent cataphracts are shock cavalry for hold-and-attack fights;
  - Theurgs have Star Fires, and Lightning Bolt once Evocation 2 is in;
  - Gift of Cheated Fate comes at Alteration 2.
- **Barbarian and lizard provinces:** bring archers or a Theurg, and a line as wide as the
  blob. Don't send a narrow wall of heavy infantry.

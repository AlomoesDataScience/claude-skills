# Magic Duel (Dominions 6, illwiki)

The user's list (2026-11-03). Source: illwiki `magic-duel` (the manual has no rules for it; check in game).

## The spell
- Evocation 3, Astral (S1), costs 1 astral pearl. Targets a random enemy mage with Astral skill on the field.
- Both roll 1d6 (NOT open-ended) + **base** Astral skill. Path boosts, items, communions, extra pearls don't count
  (extra pearls only cut fatigue). Feeblemind blocks magic; empowerment raises the base.
- Lower total dies (instakill); a tie kills BOTH. Mindless can't duel.
- So a skill gap of 6+ means the higher mage always wins; equal skill = 5/12 win, 5/12 lose, 1/6 both die.

## Using it
- Cheap S1 mages + a pearl each can trade for the enemy's expensive astral mages or an astral pretender.
- Script it early: duels resolve fast, before the target's big spells.

## Staying safe
- Keep Astral off battlefield casters you can't lose (or keep them home).
- Out-tier: a mage 6 levels above every duelist can't lose.
- Mind Blank (G1S1) / Mirror Mind (G2S1): up to 75% protection, MR check to start the duel.
- Shapechanging pretenders whose battle form has negative path modifiers bury their astral (wiki).
- Tir na n'Og: no native astral mages - the threat is only to random-path S1 mages or astral pretenders.

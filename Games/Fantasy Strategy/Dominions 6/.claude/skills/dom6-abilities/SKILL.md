# Unit attributes and abilities (Dominions 6, vanilla)

Plan item 23 (the user approved the extras 2026-11-03). Manual pp.38-41; ~500 abilities exist - right-click an
ability on the unit card (dom6-game). Numbers for a unit: dom6-inspector (vanilla), dom6-scbm (mod).

## Basic attributes (p.38)
- **Size**: 10 size points per square; supply use = size - 2 (size 1-2: 1/2, size 0: none; animals half;
  rider and mount both eat).
- Strength (damage, breaking free), Attack / Defence (melee), Protection (by body part; click it),
  Morale, MR, Precision (missiles and spells), **Encumbrance** = fatigue per round attacking (moving is
  free), Map move, Combat speed.
- **Fatigue**: unconscious at 100, HP damage past 200 (dom6-fatigue, dom6-battlefield).
- **Age / max age**: +50% max age per Death level (undead), Earth (inanimate), Blood (demons), Nature
  (others); -5% per Fire level for Nature-aged beings.
- **Leadership**: normal, undead (mages 50 per Death level, 10 per Blood level), magical (paths).

## Abilities the manual explains (pp.39-40)
| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| Ambidextrous | cuts the extra-weapon attack penalty by its level (encumbrance +1 per extra weapon stays) |
| Berserker | when wounded, morale vs 12 -> berserk, never routs; ends if knocked out; none after round 100 |
| Ethereal | 75% of non-magical strikes miss; walks through fort walls when storming; tramples for half |
| Glamour | Mirror Image in battle; invisible in friendly provinces |
| Recuperation | heals afflictions over time unless Old Age (dom6-healing) |
| Regeneration | heals HP each round, fewer afflictions; not for inanimate (Reconstruction) or undead (Reforming Flesh) |
| Awe +n | attackers need morale vs 10 + n each round to attack it; Sun Awe fails underground / dark |
| Fear +n | squads within 6 + n squares lose morale and check vs 10 (+1 per full 5 fear) or rout |
| Heat / Chill aura | heat or frost clouds L1-2 around it, 3 squares + scales |
| Poison cloud | poison clouds L1-2 around it |
| Stun | no action for about a round |
| Invisibility | -9 attack against it in melee; spirit sight sees it; the blind lose nothing more |
| Petrification | an attacker failing MR is stone for 3-6 rounds, then MR again or dies |
| Standard | +morale to its squad (best standard only) |
| Horror mark | monthly chance of a horror attack; horrors go for marked units first |
| Trample | displaces the square; each victim rolls defence - fatigue/10 vs 3d6, fails = 7 + size AP damage, else 1 |
| Swallow | trample victims removed until the swallower dies; some digest them |

## Unit classes (pp.40-41)
- **Magic beings**: need a mage's magic leadership; rout without it.
- **Mindless**: morale 50, never rout while led, dissolve without a leader; can't share a squad with others.
- **Undead**: banishable; undead leadership (Death); -1 morale in mixed squads; immune to affliction
  healing and disease effects.
- Demons and inanimate: see the unit card (holy and smite interactions: dom6-holy).

## Counters (short)
Awe and fear: high morale, standards, Sermon/Fanaticism (dom6-morale). Ethereal: magic weapons, spells.
Trample: high defence, low fatigue, big units. Invisible: spirit sight, area spells.

# Site searching (Dominions 6, vanilla)

The user's list (2026-11-03). Manual pp.28-29 (Magic Sites), turn order step 14 (p.55); illwiki `magic-sites`.

## The rules
- Sites are hidden until found (a few show on capture). Order **Search for Magic Sites** (a mage, in a
  province you own): he finds every site of HIS paths with difficulty <= his level in that path (difficulty
  1-4; level 4 finds everything of that path) (p.28). Holy sites need holy searchers.
- Whether path boosters raise the search level is not in the manual - test it before relying on it.
- More sites in forests, wastes, mountains, deep seas; fewer in plains and farmland (p.28). Game setting:
  magic site frequency (EA 65, MA 55, LA 45 default - wiki).
- Some site effects work before discovery: scale changes, disease, horror marks, holy fire/power, events
  (wiki) - a sudden disease outbreak can mean a hidden Death site.
- Gems from a site reach the treasury only with a friendly-territory link to a lab (p.79).
- National recruitment sites (Forest of Avalon) only work for their nation; others just get the gems (p.28).

## Remote searching (rituals, reveal ALL sites of a path at any level)
Augury (Fire, range 5), Auspex (Air, 2, not across sea), Voice of Apsu (Water, 3, can hit enemy land),
Voice of Tiamat (elemental, underwater), Gnome Lore (Earth, 3), Arcane Probing (Astral, 4),
Haruspex (Nature), the Death and Glamour versions, Bowl of Blood (Blood, 5), Acashic Knowledge (all paths).
Check costs and levels in dom6-spells / inspector.

## Plan
- Search every owned province once per path with the highest level you have (a level-1 search misses
  difficulty 2-4 sites: you may have to search again later with a better mage).
- Cheap mages with several paths are the best searchers; keep a list of province x path x level searched
  (the Province Chronicles screen, hotkey i, keeps track of site searches - manual).
- Rituals beat walking once you have the gems: one cast = a whole path at every level.

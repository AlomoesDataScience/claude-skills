# Scouting and intelligence (Dominions 6)

From update plan E (2026-11-03, the fog2m-scouting pattern: Dominions has REAL fog of war). Generic method:
scouting. Rules: manual p.30 (Scouting and Scrying), pp.49-51 (stealth); screen: dom6-map, dom6-game.

## What each source shows (manual p.30)
| Source in the province | Reveals |
|---|---|
| Scout (any unit there) | owner, military info, forts, province history; temperature here and next door |
| Priest | as scout + dominion strength and owner |
| Spy | as scout + income, supplies, magic sites, unrest, PD, more accurate military info |
| Your dominion | owner, income, temperature; dominion strength of neighbours |
| Scrying | owner, very accurate military info, income, supplies, sites, PD, history, dominion, forts, unrest |
| Owning it | everything; owner, **unreliable** military info and temperature of neighbours; names 1/2/3 steps out (EA/MA/LA) |

## Fog of war in practice (dom6-game, the user)
- Unseen provinces show name and terrain only. A sighting is as old as the turn it was made - write the turn.
- Scout reports are one line ("about N units ... mainly X and Y"). **Numbers can be wrong** (30 reported, 80
  there); types are rarely hidden. Bordering provinces get reports without a scout - unreliable ones.
- Independents never patrol, so scouts can sit in indie land freely; nations' patrols and PD 15+ can catch them.

## Scouts
- Stealthy scouts sneak (dom6-stealth: found if patrol + 2d25 > stealth + 2d25). Scouts are cheap: one per
  front, parked where enemy armies must pass (chokepoints, next to their forts and capital).
- A scout hidden in a province sees it better than a border report. Spies (stealthy priests / spy units) for
  income and PD; scrying spells and site searches at range when scouts can't get in.
- In battle: Retreat + far rear (they escape at once).

## Each turn's reading routine
1. Messages first (battles, scout deaths = something is there).
2. Strategic map (dom6-map): every foreign army in view -> scout report -> army log with the turn.
3. Diff against last turn: what moved, what's new, what vanished (into a fort? sneaking?).
4. Enemy reach next turn (map move, terrain, roads) -> threatened provinces.
5. Scouts to re-park; anything only scrying can answer.

## Counter-scouting
- Patrol key provinces (commanders count double; fliers good), keep PD 15+ where it matters (dom6-economy).
- Kill or chase enemy scouts; their scout dying in your land is information for them too.
- Move big armies where reports are border-only; split a feint from the real attack (screening).

# Blood magic and blood slaves (Dominions 6, vanilla)

The user's list (2026-11-03). Manual pp.51-52; illwiki `blood-hunting`, `blood-magic`,
`generic-blood-magic-guide` (Wigglefig). Tir na n'Og has no native blood (SCBM: check dom6-scbm first).

## Getting slaves
Blood hunting: see **dom6-blood-hunting** (checks, slaves found, unrest, patrollers, retaliation).

## Slaves as a resource
- Blood slaves are the gems of the Blood path: spell costs, forging, empowerment, rituals. They're units in
  the province - they must be carried to the army or lab.
- Gem-cost blood spells **can't be used underwater**; offensive blood mostly fails on undead/inanimate.
- **Sabbath** = the blood communion (Sabbath Master / Sabbath Slave, B1, blood mages only); it works like
  the astral communion (dom6-communions: path boost, shared fatigue, helpless slaves).

## Notable spells (wiki table, by research level, vanilla)
- B1: Blood Burst (AN magic damage, square), Blood Heal, Reinvigoration (wipes all fatigue - script it),
  Summon Imps, Bind Fiery Imps, Bind Shadow Imp (stealthy commander).
- B2: Bowl of Blood (site search at range), Banish Demon, Agony (fear), Hell Power (+4 att/def/str/prec/prot/
  morale, +2 MR and paths, but a horror mark), Bind Spine Devil / Fiend.
- B3: Leeching Touch, Pain Transfer (half damage to nearby slaves), Blood Feast (cures afflictions, kills
  population), Bind Devil / Frost Fiend, Infernal Circle (+1 range to blood rituals).
- Higher: the big demon binds, Blood Rain, Blood Moon, curses - check dom6-spells.

## Strategy (wiki guide)
- Strength: scales with population, not site luck; any commander can hunt; empowering in blood is cheap.
- Costs: unrest and population (income), patrol upkeep, mages hunting instead of researching, the separate
  Blood research school, slave logistics.
- Bootstrapping: high-blood pretender, scouts as poor hunters, indie mages with random blood, sites
  (Citadel of the Lore Masters etc.), events.
- Against a blood nation: their income is their hunting provinces - raid them, raise unrest, kill hunters.

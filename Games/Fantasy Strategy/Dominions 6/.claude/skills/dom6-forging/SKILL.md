# Forging items (Dominions 6, vanilla)

The user's list (2026-11-03). Manual pp.79-80 (Magic Items); turn order step 5 (forged items are ready that
same turn, p.55); illwiki `construction`, `forge-bonus`. Item lists and rankings: dom6-items.

## The rules
- Order **Forge Item**: a mage in a province with a lab; needs the Construction level, the item's paths
  and its gems (from the pool) (p.80). The item lands in the national treasury (F7/F8) the same turn.
- Tiers (wiki): Construction 1 trinkets, 3 lesser, 5 greater, 7 powerful, 9 **artifacts** (unique: one in the
  world). Artifacts start "yearning" (half cost) after Construction 9 is researched by anyone, Forge of the
  Ancients, or the Throne of Creation/Artificer is claimed (+50% chance a month each) (p.80).
- Repeat: select the mage and shift + the forge key to forge the same item every turn (p.80).
- National items (dark blue background) only for that nation; discounted ones grey (p.81).
- Forge bonus / Master Smith (ability, Forge of the Ancients global): cheaper items (wiki forge-bonus).
- Boosters only work in paths the user already has (p.74).

## What to forge first (general)
1. Path boosters that unlock key spells or communions for your mages.
2. Thug and supercombatant kits (dom6-thugs, dom6-supercombatants).
3. Gem-makers / income items if the game is long.
4. Bodyguard and survival items for important mages (MR, arrow protection).

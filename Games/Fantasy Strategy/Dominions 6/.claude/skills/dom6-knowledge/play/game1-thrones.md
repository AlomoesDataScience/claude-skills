# Game 1 thrones of ascension (F9, read turn 2)

100 ascension points to win. 60 thrones: 10 of level 3, 20 of level 2, 30 of level 1 = 100 points in all,
so winning by thrones alone means holding every one (unless some are destroyed or conquered away).
Left-click a throne in F9 for its effects. Gem colours are icons (not read); guess them from the name.

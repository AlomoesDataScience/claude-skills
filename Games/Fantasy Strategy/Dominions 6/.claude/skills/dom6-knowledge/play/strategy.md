# Strategy the user teaches

General Dominions 6 strategy lessons from the user, for planning games

## Research

- Research time in mage-turns = level cost / research ability. Cost (user's estimate): 50 RP for level 1, doubling each level (level 4 = 750 RP = 57 Theurg-turns). One mage a turn from the capital gives 55 mage-turns by turn 10. Estimate when each spell arrives from the mages' research values and the recruit plan. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Recruit the best research-per-turn mage at the capital (Pythium: the Theurg), not the cheapest: cheap auxiliaries (Acolytes) almost halve research speed there; build them elsewhere. Later, research comes from other forts and the capital makes big mages (Arch Theurgs). *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Can't recruit only research mages: the capital slot must also make commanders (army leaders, scouts, replacements) until other forts take that over. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- In serious games gems go into research items (Owl Quill +6, Lightless Lantern +12): the fastest way to ramp research. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Expansion and building

- Expansion runs turns 1-20; aim for at least 1 conquest a turn on average in a first game (skilled players far more). First new fort plus second lab/temple about turns 8-12; plan later forts and labs. Get basic counters to whatever is met early. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Few strong armies: three armies by turn 3 is far too fast; none would have meaningful numbers. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Magic

- Communion lets a nation cast any spell the communion reaches, so boosters matter less for communion nations. Dispel is situational and won't win the game. Keep the ability to pivot from conjuration to strong battle magic. Alchemy converts gems. Plan items as well as spells (early/mid/late). *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Pythium

- Big ritual-cast nation and great battle communions. Acolytes, Theurgs and Arch Theurgs are all Grand Communicants; 3 Theurgs are enough for the S5 globals; Arcane Nexus over Stellar Focus; Wrath of God is good. Sky Metal Matrix (E1 S1) for the god: empower an astral mage in Earth to forge it. Gladiators leave after getting hit. Slingers only vs unarmoured; hire indie archers. Full plan: plans/ma-pythium-research.md. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Battle magic

- Battle spells cannot be aimed at a chosen target: most miss the intended unit and hit masses of troops, so area spells beat single-target ones (Mind Burn rarely kills). Communion lowers fatigue for heavy spells like Thunder Strike. Orb Lightning can hit your own men without shock resistance. Body Ethereal is a great buff for point units; Mistform is really good; Gift of Flight lets thugs hit the rear. Thunderstorm can be cast on enemy armies outside battle. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Thrones

- Default victory: hold enough Thrones of Ascension. A throne must be claimed (Claim Throne order) by a pretender, prophet or level-3 priest; a claimed throne spreads dominion like a temple (1-7 checks). *(manual p. 84, 2026-10-27)*

## The god

- A dead god can be resummoned: priests on Call God give 1 point per priest level a turn; about 50 points brings it back (it may lose a magic level or dominion). *(user + manual p. 36, 2026-10-27)*
- Magic Duel (S1, Evo 3): an astral mage challenges another; each rolls d6 + unmodified astral level (communion and boosters don't count); the loser dies, a tie kills both. So don't give the god a low Astral level, and use our own high-Astral mages (or S1 kamikazes) against valuable enemy astral mages. *(user + inspector, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Dominion 7 is high when the bless is weak (weak sacreds, not needed early); 5 bless points is fine. Imprisoned gives points for another path but the god only arrives mid to late game. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Blesses for a communion nation: Unbreakable only pays off when mages get hurt and keep going (a regeneration turbo-communion build); without regeneration, Resilient (+hp, stackable) is better. Reinvigoration gives +1 fatigue recovery; high-level communion spells generally overpower it, so take 1-2 at most. Unaging fits old mages. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Underwater

- Plans must handle the ocean. Only amphibious/aquatic units enter sea provinces; others need water breathing (Ring of Water Breathing W1 for one commander; Pills of Water Breathing A2 for size 75 of troops). Piercing weapons keep their attack underwater; slashing/blunt lose attack equal to length. Many spells don't work underwater: check the inspector's uwok flag. Wrath of God doesn't reach underwater or caves. *(user + manual + inspector, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Swimming (Hydras, Hydra Tamer) only crosses rivers; it is not amphibious. Items are the best way under water. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*

## Fatigue and communions

- Spell fatigue = cost / (1 + skill above minimum), communion levels included; plus caster enc + 2x armour enc per cast. Communion: +n levels for 2^n slaves; fatigue split over master + all slaves, x1/÷2/x2/x4 by slave level vs master (below half = x4). Slaves keep taking fatigue past 100 and take damage from 200; master death gives every slave 3d50 fatigue. Details and worked numbers: plans/ma-pythium-research.md. *(manual pp. 66, 76, 78-79, 2026-10-27)*

## Army

- Communion slaves are expendable when plentiful (Pythium Communicants), but they don't fight: only make them for a master with many good spells. Independent troops and mages can be bought; archers are the most common and beat national slingers. Pythium gladiators leave after about one battle. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Capital-circle provinces can hold huge independent garrisons (the user's elf game: 2 provinces of 120 Barbarians) that must be taken. Hydras' poison aura hurts their own neighbours: use them as auxiliaries or a raiding force, not in the main army. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Assassins can be made into thugs with items (stealth + magic weapons + survival gear like an ethereal robe). The pretender's spells can handle Hydras' poison (poison protection). *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Assassination: the target is a random enemy commander in the province; the assassin fights him plus 1-5 reinforcements (bodyguards 50% each, townsfolk, guards). The target is unprepared and follows no script. *(user + manual p. 52, 2026-10-27)*
- Assassin and other thug kits: prioritise survival; the assassin's poison dagger is enough offence. Ethereal is weak against targets with magic (magic weapons and spells make it moot). Earth mages make the good gear (blacksteel); in a pinch Enchanted Ring Mail + Enchanted Helmet + Rabbit Foot Charm make a con-1 thug. *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Independent mages of the path you lack can't simply be hired; you get them from summons (earth: Summon Cave Kobolds needs G+E, Troll King's Court E4). Pythium gets nature mages natively from Enchantment (Awaken Hamadryad). *(user, 2026-10-27, 2026-10-27)*
- Squads: up to 3 per commander; the Army Setup shows 2/3 = squads used / allowed. Past 3 squads the squads take a morale penalty. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- A commander with no magic who is just a leader (e.g. Pythium's start Centurion) should get the battle order Stay behind troops: he loiters behind the lines, safer than fighting. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Pythium start army (turn 1): 25 Alae Legionnaires and 20 Velites (javelins range 10, morale 10, no Skirmisher) under Theocratus (leadership 100: +1 morale for up to 3 squads). Set up: Legionnaires in Line formation at the front, Velites in Box right behind them, Theocratus further back on Stay behind troops. *(Claude, 2026-10-27)*
- Javelins have 2 ammo, and a squad on Fire attacks once out of missiles, so squads with javelins (Pythium's Alae Legionnaires as well as Velites; the Legionnaires also carry spears, the Velites are lighter) should usually Fire first. Turn 1 setup: both squads Fire, closest enemy. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Scouts: battle order Retreat, and in Position Army right-click them to the far rear (left edge): from there they escape immediately. *(user, 2026-10-27)*

## Expansion

- Name a prophet on turn 1 or 2 and let him expand for you. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Turn 1 recruiting (the user): set up the first expansion army: one commander, or a mage if the starting commander will do, then lots of survivable troops so expansion takes no attrition, plus one Standard per squad (2-3). Pythium: Alae Legionnaire (spear: repels, length) or Hastatus (short sword: +2 damage, no repels); skip Battle Vestals; Gladiators only to crack a province you otherwise can't; Slingers situational. Always take the Theurg Communicant (max 1/month). Assassin expansion is possible but not for a first game. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Pythium turn 1 actual buy: Theurg (repeat recruitment on for commanders), 2 Standards, 6 Alae Legionnaires, 1 Theurg Communicant. Resources (165) are the binding limit, not gold; 150 gold left over. *(Claude, 2026-10-27)*
- Naming a prophet on turn 1 is standard: in game 1, 16 of 37 gods (Pythium included) proclaimed one on turn 1. *(user + game, 2026-10-27)*
- Prophet choice: if the starting commander is poor (Pythium's Centurion) or better options exist, many players recruit a nicer commander on turn 1 and make him prophet on turn 2. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Clear the capital's ring of neighbours first: neighbouring provinces feed a fort (resources), so the capital can recruit more. Captured provinces can recruit their local commanders: the panel's 'Recruitment points: 90 / 1' is recruitment points / commander points. A unit the province lacks resources for still queues (dimmed) and is built over the following turns (manual p. 42); gold is taken at once. *(user + manual, 2026-10-27)*
- Get a second expansion army out ASAP: recruit another commander at the capital as soon as there are enough troops for it. Early is okay, late is not. Reinforcing the expansion armies is also okay. When the capital's commander points cannot cover both a Theurg (2 CP) and a commander, the commander wins that month. *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Correction to the above: recruit the second commander when the capital has enough troops for a real second army (about 40 for turn-3 targets of 60-80 indies), not before; ~20 troops is not enough. Commanders are also needed later to carry reinforcements to the front. *(user, 2026-10-27)*

## Scouting the enemy

- The nation info screen (click a pretender in F4) ends with 'X worships the pretender NAME, TITLES'. The titles (epithets) come from the god's design: e.g. Lady of Order = Order scale, Vessel of Might = strength/earth-ish. Worth checking against a dangerous neighbour; not needed in a first game. *(user, 2026-10-27)*

## Economy

- Turn 1: spend all the gold. From turn 2-3 on there are reasons to keep some (the user will show them). *(user, 2026-10-27)*
- Resources are not stored: spend every province's resources every turn (gold carries over). Mercenary bids are a one-time payment for 3 months. *(the user, game 1 turn 5, 2026-10-27)*
- Save gold for buildings: temples and forts cost a lot, and armies cost maintenance every turn (click Income in the top bar to see it). Aim to have a fort under construction by about turn 10. Don't spend all gold every turn after turn 1; spend all resources. *(the user, game 1 after turn 5, 2026-10-27)*
- Get the first new fort started by the end of year 1 (turn 12) at the latest; turn ~8-10 is good. Pythium Palisades cost 1000 gold, 5 months. *(the user, game 1 turn 6, 2026-10-28)*

## Battle-magic

- Prefer AoE spells over single-target ones: an AoE spell still lands among the enemy when it misses its target. Slime (W1, Evo 1, AoE 1, -2 att/def and slowed) is the early Theurg spam spell; stronger slimes come later. Cold Bolt (W2) is another option. *(user, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*
- Main order for mages: Stay behind troops rather than Cast spells (user's habit); keep the protective opener (Air Shield, Twist Fate) before the spam spell. *(user, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*
- Pythium battle communion: one master (Bartholomeus, H4 S3) with the Theurg Communicants as his Guard commander bodyguard squad (they auto-cast Communion Slave). Master script: Communion Master, one Divine Blessing (blesses all sacreds: Theurgs and Communicants), then Star Fires (damage grows with the boosted path level). Don't make the other Theurgs slaves or masters for now. *(user, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*
- Trueshot (A1) on javelin troops may be worth it (+4 precision). Unknown whether Sermon of Courage stacks with Fanaticism; casting both is fine. *(user, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*
- Research: get Alteration up fast for Body Ethereal (Alt 3, S1; very good for mages), and Construction early. Battle mages matter later; don't overthink scripts yet, but rescript after each research level and before big battles. Scripts copy: hover a commander's script bar in Army Setup, ctrl+1..9; hover another's bar, press the number. *(user, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*
- Kills per cast, simulated (3 men per square, the 10-size-point rule; manual missile hit roll, so shields count double unless the spell ignores them). Vs Militia / Heavy Inf / Barbarian / mages: Theurg Star Fires S2 0.08/0.07/0.12/0.4; Bartholomeus with 5 slaves: Star Fires S5 0.15/0.15/0.26/0.7, Cold Bolt W4 (15 AP) 0.21/0.11/0.41/1.0, Lightning Bolt A4 0.40/0.40/0.34/0.4, Cold Blast W4 (3 sq, range 11) 0.7/0.4/1.5/3, Falling Frost W4 (14 sq) 2.1/0.8/3.1/~all. Baseline: an Alae Legionnaire at Murias made 2.0 damage and 0.13 kills per attack, about 0.4 kills per battle. Point bolts lose to shields (parry x2 in the hit roll); AoE and shield-ignoring spells win. Water Strike and Gooey Water are underwater-only. *(Claude's simulation, 2026-10-28; Murias battle review, 2026-10-28)*
- Evocation ranking (dom6-spells evocations.md): Pythium's best are Ice Strike (W2 Evo7, Bartholomeus without communion, 5-7 kills a cast against light troops), Niefel Flames (W5 Evo9), Falling Frost (Comm, Evo5), Storm Wind (A2 Evo3: knocks down 17-22 men for 10 fatigue), Stellar Cascades (Theurgs S2 Evo5: 25 fatigue to ~12 men). Arcane Bolt hurts only magic beings; Solar Rays and Cleansing Water only undead/demons; Astral Geyser, Orb Lightning, Star Fires are weak. Single bolts miss thin mage lines at range: big areas and many projectiles kill mages. *(Claude's model, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*

## Targeting in battle

- Arrows and spells can hit our mages, but the enemy can't aim at commanders directly: a commander is hit only by luck (a stray shot into his square) or when he's the only target left. So mage protection guards against strays and the end of a lost fight, not aimed fire. *(the user, 2026-10-28, 2026-10-28)*

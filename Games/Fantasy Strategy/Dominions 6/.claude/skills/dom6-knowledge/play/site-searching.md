# Site searching: expected finds by terrain and searcher level

## Dominions 6 numbers (computed 2026-10-28)

Made by `scripts/siteodds.py` from the inspector's Dom6 site list: the 900 random sites (rarity 0-2) with their path, level, terrain mask and gems. The generation procedure is the Dom5 one (below). Checked against the Dom5 chart: where the site list didn't change (Deep Sea water, earth, holy) the model gives the chart's numbers exactly.

How to read a cell: `sites L1/L2/L3 · gems L1/L2/L3` = expected sites of that path (and gems a month from them) a searcher of path level 1, 2 or 3 finds in one unsearched province of that terrain. A remote search ritual (Haruspex, Gnome Lore, Arcane Probing...) finds every site of its path, so at least the L3 column. "Rolls" is the expected number of random sites of all paths in the province (level-0 sites are found on conquest).

Frequencies 60/50/40 match the Dom5 chart's EA/MA/LA defaults. **Dom6's own default** (manual, command line `--magicsites`) is 40; check the game's setting. The numbers scale almost linearly with frequency. Dom6 has no Highland terrain mask, so Highland is left out.

What changed from Dom5:
- **Glamour sites are new** (109 random sites). They took share from every path.
- **Nature dropped about 15-20%** (EA forest 0.34/0.41/0.43 → 0.28/0.34/0.37), most in swamps, seas and caves.
- **Earth in caves dropped by a third** (EA 0.93 → 0.63 at E1), and in mountains ~20%.
- Fire, air, astral and death dropped ~10-30%; water stayed about the same.
- Forests are now the best land for nature and among the best for gems overall.

## Dominions 5 chart (transcribed)


Transcribed 2026-10-28 from the three charts on illwiki's Magic Sites page
(https://illwiki.com/magic-sites; images in `img/`), which the user pointed to. Source: research by SirToma and Loggy, compiled by Isitaris.
**These are Dominions 5 numbers**: Dom6 added Glamour sites, so the Dom6 split will differ somewhat. Use them for comparisons.

How to read a cell: `sites L1/L2/L3 · gems L1/L2/L3` = expected number of sites of that path (and expected gems a month from them) that a searcher with path level 1, 2 or 3 finds in one unsearched province with only that terrain, at the age's default site frequency.
A remote search ritual (Haruspex, Gnome Lore, Arcane Probing...) finds **every** site of its path whatever the level, so it gets at least the L3 column.

Site generation (same page): each province rolls up to 8 times for a site; the first roll's chance is global frequency + terrain modifier (Waste +20, Cave +15, Forest/Mountain/Swamp +10, Highland +5, Farmland −20, Plains/Sea +0, Deep Sea +10; 'many sites' +25, throne +20), each further roll 75% of the previous. Rarity 0/1/2 sites come 3/4, 9/40, 1/40 of the time.

### Reading it

- **Level matters less than terrain.** Going from a level-1 to a level-3 searcher adds about 20-30% more finds in most cells; the terrain changes them several-fold.
- **Earth lives in caves** (about 1 site a cave province at Earth 1 in EA) and mountains/highlands (~0.4-0.5); forests and farms have few.
- **Nature lives in forests, swamps and seas** (EA forest 0.34 / 0.41 / 0.43 sites at N1/N2/N3).
- **Wastes and swamps** are the richest provinces overall; farmland the poorest.
- Water's and Fire's level-3 columns jump: many of their sites are level 3.
- An N2 searcher already finds about 95% of the level ≤3 nature sites; a remote ritual's edge over him is reach (3+ provinces away, no walking), not finds.
- MA's Forest average (2.00/2.40/2.61) is identical to EA's on the chart; probably a copy slip in the source image.

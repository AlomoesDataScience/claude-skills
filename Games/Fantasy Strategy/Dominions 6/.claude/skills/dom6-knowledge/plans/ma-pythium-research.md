dominion 6, Order 3 Prod 2 **Magic 1**, blesses Unaging + Reinvigoration + Resilient ×3 (8 of 8),

## What the user taught (2026-10-27)

- Pythium is a **big ritual-cast nation** and **great at battle communions**: any spell the
  communion reaches can be cast. Mind how communions work (slave fatigue, see below).
- **Grand Communion**: Theurg Acolytes are Grand Communicants too, and 3 Theurgs are enough for
  the S5 globals. Arcane Nexus is within reach (better than Stellar Focus).
- **Dispel** is situational and won't win the game. **Boosters** don't matter much, because
  communion does the job. **Conjuration** is good, but keep the ability to pivot to strong battle
  magic. **Wrath of God** is good. **Buffs** are good. **Alchemy** converts gems.
- **Sky Metal Matrix** (C5, E1 S1) for the god: the god then commands a communion. Forging it
  needs an astral mage empowered in Earth.
- **Gladiators** leave after getting hit.
- **Slingers** are situational: only against unarmoured or lightly armoured enemies. Hire
  independent archers instead.
- Get basic **counters** to anything met early.
- **Research**: cost ÷ research ability = mage-turns. The user's estimate: 50 RP for level 1,
  doubling per level, so level 4 = 750 RP = 57 Theurg-turns. One mage a turn from the capital
  gives 55 mage-turns by turn 10.
- **Expansion** runs turns 1-20, at an average of at least 1 conquest per turn for a first game.
  The first new fort plus the second lab/temple come around turns 8-12.

How far each kind of spell can reach:

- **Battle communion** (combat spells). Each master gains +n in all his paths for 2^n slaves.
  - A Theurg with 4 slaves is S4 A3 W3; with 8 slaves S5 A4 W4.
  - An Arch Theurg with 8 slaves is S6 A5 W4, and F3 if he rolled Fire.
  - Slave fatigue: each participant takes cost ÷ participants. That is ×2 when the slave's level
    is below the master's, and ×4 below half of it. Communicants (no paths) take ×4, so give them
    cheap spells or put Acolytes in as slaves. Learn the rest in play.
- **Ordinary rituals**: own skill only.
  - Arch Theurg: S3 (S4 with an Astral roll), A2 (A3 with an Air roll), F1 with a Fire roll.
  - God: E4 N5.
- **Global rituals and Dispel**: Grand Communion sums the skills of Acolytes, Theurgs and Arch
  Theurgs, so every astral global is reachable, Arcane Nexus included (S8).
- **The god with a Sky Metal Matrix** becomes a communion master: with 4 slaves it is E6 N7.
- **Native nature mages** (user): **Awaken Hamadryad** (national, N4, Ench 5, 25N, cast by the
  god) gives a Hamadryad, N3 with research 7. Awaken Treelord (N5, Ench 7, 35N) gives an N4-N5
  Treelord. They add Nature casters, forgers (nature items) and research.

## Battle magic notes from the user (2026-10-27)

- **Spells can't be aimed at chosen targets.** Most shots miss the intended unit and land on
  masses of troops, so area spells against packed troops are worth more than single-target ones.
- **Mind Burn** is single-target: it might kill troops but likely won't. It is a niche
  mage-killer, not a priority.
- **Thunder Strike** (A3, Evo 4) belongs on the communion-backed mages: communion cuts its
  fatigue (50).
- **Orb Lightning** is good, but without shock resistance it can hit our own men.
- **Body Ethereal** (Alt 3) is a fantastic buff on a point unit (size-1 effect).
- **Mistform** (A2, Alt 4) is really good: the main thing the second draft missed.
- **Gift of Flight** (A2, Ench 3) lets thugged-out units attack the enemy's rear.
- **Thunderstorm** (A4 ritual, Evo 7, 30A, range 3) is sometimes worth casting on enemy armies
  outside battle. A4 is out of native reach: an Air-rolled Arch Theurg (A3) needs an Air
  booster. Hurricane (A3 ritual, Evo 4, range 5) is the version an Air-rolled Arch Theurg can
  cast.

## How to win

The default victory is **Thrones of Ascension**: hold enough ascension points (level 1-3
thrones are worth 1-3). A throne must be **claimed** to count and to spread dominion, and only a
pretender, a prophet or a **level-3 priest** can claim it (manual p. 84). The Arch Theurg is H3,
so every Arch Theurg can claim thrones; Bartholomeus (H4) too. If the game is set to Conquer
All, the same plan runs longer and ends with killing the other gods.

1. **Turns 1-20, the snowball.** Expand at least one province a turn with two, then three,
   strong legion armies. A Theurg every turn for research. Fort, lab and temple on turns 8-12.
   Take the nearest level-1 throne as soon as an army can beat its guards, with the god or an
   Arch Theurg along to claim it.
2. **Turns 20-40, out-fight neighbours.** Battle communions (Theurgs + Communicants/Acolytes)
   with Lightning Bolt, Thunder Strike and Stellar Cascades (and the god's Blade Wind) behind legions buffed with Body
   Ethereal and Mistform. Angels of the Host as flying holy troops. The god with the Sky Metal
   Matrix becomes the strongest caster. Take and claim more thrones; each throne also spreads
   our dominion.
3. **Turns 30+, globals.** Grand Communions cast The Wrath of God (lightning on every enemy in
   our dominion, which thrones, temples and the H2-H3 Theurgs keep spreading), then Arcane Nexus.
   Keep the Grand Communion at home ready to dispel a global that really hurts.
4. **Hold what is claimed.** Thrones attract attacks: garrison the claimed ones, use Teleport and
   Gateway (Thaum 4-6) to move communions to threatened thrones, and fight with magic where the
   enemy masses its troops.
5. **Finish.** Hold the required ascension points for the needed time, or, if neighbours
   collapse, push into their capitals where their dominion is weakest.

Main risks: an early rush before the communions work; assassins and missiles killing communion
masters (Mistform, Protective Winds, bodyguards); losing the god to a thug before it has the
Matrix; spending too many turns on research and too few on conquest.

## The god dies: Call God

Priests on the Call God order give 1 point per priest level a turn. At about 50 points the god
returns to the capital, but it may lose a magic level or a point of dominion (manual p. 36).
Four Theurgs (H2 each) bring it back in about 6 turns, and Arch Theurgs (H3) faster. Plan
Theurgs for this rather than giving up when the god falls. The Sky Metal Matrix stays where the
god died, so recover it.

## The ocean (user: "Underwater.")

The facts (inspector and manual):
- Only amphibious or aquatic units can enter sea provinces. **Nobody in Pythium is**: the Hydras'
  and the Hydra Tamer's Swimming only crosses rivers (user). The best way under water is items
  (user).
- Water breathing:
  - **Ring of Water Breathing** (W1, C3, 5W): one commander. Any Theurg can forge it.
  - **Pills of Water Breathing** (A2, C5, 10A): the bearer plus size 75 of troops (about 25
    legionnaires). The Arch Theurg forges it.
  - The Manual of Water Breathing (N3 W1) needs one forger with both paths: none.
- Under water, slashing and blunt weapons lose attack equal to their length. Piercing weapons
  don't, and mixed ones lose half. Spears and short swords are fine; flails (Gladiators) are bad.
- Spells that **work** under water (uwok): Communion, Lightning Bolt, Shock Wave, Orb Lightning,
  Astral Geyser, Arcane Bolt, Paralyze, Soul Slay, Body Ethereal, Mistform, Light of the Northern
  Star, Power of the Spheres, Astral Shield, Spell Ward, Antimagic, Quickness, Water Shield, Ice
  Shield, Mass Regeneration, Haste, Teleport.
- Spells that **don't**: Thunder Strike, Stellar Cascades, Solar Rays, Star Fires, Protective
  Winds, Gift of Flight, Storm, Wrathful Skies, Solar Brilliance, Cold Blast, Falling Frost.
  Rituals like Contact Angel of the Host cannot be cast there either.
- The Wrath of God does not strike enemies under water or in caves.

The decision:
1. **Turns 1-20: stay on land.** The sea is slow to take and the land expansion pays first. Coastal
   provinces get a modest guard. Land-locked sea nations struggle on land, and our communions
   fight best there.
2. **Construction 3-5 decides when we go in**: Rings of Water Breathing on 2-3 Theurgs and the
   commanders; then
3. a communion (ringed Theurgs plus Acolytes) goes in with a Pills legion (step 4), taking coastal
   sea provinces next to home, especially any with a throne or sites. Its spells under water: Astral Geyser, Lightning Bolt,
   Orb Lightning, Paralyze, Soul Slay; buffs Body Ethereal, Mistform, Quickness, Water Shield.
4. **Construction 5**: Pills of Water Breathing give a legion of about 25 spearmen or
   short-swordsmen (Hastati, Principes, Triarii) for a real sea army. The god needs a Ring too.
5. **If a sea nation is a neighbour and attacks**: fight it on land first (sea troops are weaker
   ashore); go under water only to finish it, or for a throne, with a ringed communion + a Pills
   legion (Hydras need a Pills bearer too).
6. Under water, the war is decided by astral and lightning magic, not by globals. The Wrath of
   God doesn't reach there, so hold the sea throne with troops.

## Fatigue and communion rules (manual pp. 66, 76, 78-79)

- **Fatigue**: at 100 a unit falls unconscious and regains 5 a round while out. At 200 more
  fatigue becomes hit-point damage. Every 10 fatigue is -1 defence, every 20 is -1 attack.
- **Spell fatigue** = the spell's cost ÷ (1 + skill above the spell's minimum).
  - One extra level halves it, two extra cut it to a third.
  - Communion levels count, so a big communion makes spells cheap.
- Each cast also costs the caster his **encumbrance + 2× his armour's encumbrance**, which no
  skill reduces. Mages must stay out of heavy armour.
- **Communion**:
  - The master gets +n levels in every path he has for 2^n slaves (2 → +1, 4 → +2, 8 → +3,
    16 → +4). The bonus drops as soon as the slaves fall below the threshold.
  - Each cast's fatigue is split over all participants (the master plus every slave on the
    field), then changed by each slave's level: equal to the master's ×1, above ÷2, below ×2,
    **below half ×4**.
  - Communicants (no paths) always take ×4. Acolytes (S1) next to a boosted S4+ master probably
    also take ×4.
  - **Slaves don't drop out at 100.** They keep soaking fatigue past unconsciousness, and from
    200 it becomes damage. Communicants have 10 hp, so a long casting spree kills them.
    That is fine: they are cheap and plentiful, so losing them is an acceptable cost (user).
    But they don't fight, so only make them for a master who can cast a lot of good spells.
  - If every master dies or flees, each slave is stunned and takes **3d50 fatigue**.
  - Slaves get the master's self-buffs (range-0, single-target spells).
  - Communion spells take 25% longer to cast.
- Worked numbers. Master enc 3, no armour. "Per slave" assumes Communicants (×4).

| Communion | Spell (cost) | Master skill | Fatigue per cast | Per slave | Master |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Theurg + 8 | Astral Geyser (20, S3) | S5 | 6.7 | ~3 | ~4 |
| Theurg + 8 | Thunder Strike (50, A3) | A4 | 25 | ~11 | ~6 |
| Arch Theurg + 8 | Thunder Strike | A5 | 16.7 | ~7 | ~5 |
| Arch Theurg + 16 | Thunder Strike | A6 | 12.5 | ~3 | ~4 |

- **What this means for us**:
  1. More slaves both raise the level and cut each slave's share: grow toward 16.
  2. Script cheap spells and a limited number of heavy ones (Thunder Strike) so slaves stay
     under about 150.
  3. Guard the masters (backlash).
  4. No armour on mages.
  5. Theurgs as slaves of an Arch Theurg take less (×2-4) than Communicants, but they are
     research mages: Communicants and Acolytes are the slave stock.

regeneration "turbo communion" build where injured mages keep going; we have no regeneration. The legion is not sacred.

## Big independent garrisons in the capital circle

The user's elf game has two capital-circle provinces with 120 Barbarians each, which must be
taken. Expect the same here.

Barbarian (inspector 139/140): hp 13, maul (two-handed, 24 damage), body armour 9, **no helmet**,
def 8-11, morale 10, no shield.

Odds per attack (dom6-mechanics combat_calc.py, ignoring fatigue and position):

| Attacker → defender | Hit | Damage per attack |
|---|---|---|
| Barbarian → Alae Legionnaire (tower shield) | 18% (86% on the shield) | 0.31 |
| Alae Legionnaire spear → Barbarian | 62% | 0.63 |
| Emerald Guard broad sword → Barbarian | 86% | 1.70 |
| Barbarian → Emerald Guard | 11% | 0.05 |

So tower-shielded legions out-trade them badly; the danger is **fatigue** (legionnaires gain enc
7-9 per attack) and being surrounded.

The plan for such a province:
- 40+ heavy infantry with Emerald Guard in front, so the wall lasts.
- Indie archers: Barbarians have no shields and only leather, so missiles work. This is also the
  case where slingers are fine.
- If the first fight is too close: add an early communion (a Theurg with 4 slaves is A3 S4) with
  Shock Wave, Lightning Bolt and Astral Geyser, to thin the mass before contact.
- Take it before turn 10-12 if possible: it blocks the capital circle and the second fort.

## Assassin thug (user's idea)

The Assassin (80 gold) is a commander with Stealthy 65 and Assassin. How assassination works
(manual p. 52 + user):
- The target is a **random** enemy commander in the province; we can't pick one.
- The assassin fights him plus **1-5 reinforcements**: assigned bodyguards (50% chance each,
  less with assassin patience), townsfolk and city guards depending on the location.
- The target is unprepared and follows no battle script, so mages don't get their scripted buffs. Base stats: hp 10, att 13,
def 14, prec 13, short sword + poison dagger, Ambidextrous 2 (good with two weapons), leather.

What makes it work (user): **survival first**. His own poison dagger (and short sword) is
enough offence against most targets. Ethereal (Robe of Shadows) is weak here, because many
targets and reinforcements have magic, and magic makes ethereal moot.

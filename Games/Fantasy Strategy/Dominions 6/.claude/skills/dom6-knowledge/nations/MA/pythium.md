## Description

The Emerald Empire of Pythium is a splinter empire of the great Empire of Ermor. As dark arts were practiced in the heart of the Empire, the stars warned the Theurgs of Pythia to flee the impending doom. They led Pythia away from the falling Empire and formed an imperial theocracy under their wise rule. But this was not enough to save them. As Ermor crumbled, the provinces of Pythium rebelled and broke away from their old masters. The Emperor of Pythium could not reclaim the rebelling lands and the power of the Emerald Empire diminished over the centuries. The war machine of Pythium is based on the old legionnaire army of Ermor with some minor modifications. During Ermorian times, part of the Pythian lands were under the sway of the Sauromatian tribes. The Androphags used hydras of the Pythian Marshes in warfare. For a long time hydras were only used ed in the Pythian arenas, but now the old practice has been adopted along with the usage of serpent mounts. National G-catures

## National features

- **Race:** Humans
- **Military:** Legionnaires, serpent cataphracts, gladiators, hydras
- **Magic:** Astral, Air, Water, some Fire. High magic gem income.
- **Priests:** Powerful
- **Scales & Blesses:** Order limit +1
- **Buildings:** Standard Forts, starts with a Fortified City

## From the community wiki (illwiki, distilled 2026-10-28)

- **Sources:**
  - The Dom6 page (`wiki.py show pythium-ma`) has only stat tables; its strategy sections are
    empty.
  - The **Dom5** page (`wiki.py show dom5/pythium-ma`) has player commentary on every unit.
    Costs and some stats changed in Dom6, but the roles hold.
- **Strengths:**
  - huge, fast communions (every national mage communicates, and the Theurg Communicant is a
    recruitable slave, one a turn, needs a lab);
  - strong air and astral combat magic;
  - very good battlefield buffs;
  - strong national summons (angels).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - slow, expensive expansion with no sacred expansion troops;
  - weak to early rushes;
  - astral magic is weak against big PD dumps;
  - mages need a lab and a temple in the fort;
  - no early thug chassis;
  - no national nature mage to Contact Lar (find N1 independents);
  - no national mage for Crystal Matrix.
- **Unit roles (Dom5 commentary):**
  - **Retiarius**: the mainstay of expansion and rushes (net + very high piercing damage).
    With Fog Warriors, Will of the Fates and Mass Flight they're "weapons of mass destruction".
  - **Gladiator**: two attacks, good against shielded, low-protection troops.
  - **Velites** expand early; **Alae and Hastati** replace them as the line.
  - **Triarius** isn't worth it (old, encumbrance 10). **Battle Vestals** only become useful
    under big communion buffs.
  - **Emerald Guard**:
    - **the answer to assassins**: give 1-3 to a Theurg Acolyte on Bodyguard;
    - raiding: 6 per commander, Hold and Attack, Body Ethereal on them.
  - **Serpent Cataphracts**: buffed with **Body Ethereal + Luck**, 12-20 clear almost any PD.
  - **Theurg Acolyte**: research, the Body Ethereal / Luck fluffer, raid leader. In a communion
    he spams Soul Slay or Enslave Mind. Very weak to assassins.
  - **Theurg**: a strong buff caster inside communions. Outside, with the Light of the Northern
    Star, he spams Soul Slay.
  - **Arch Theurg**: every random path has a use; **an astral random is the most valuable**
    (can climb to S6+, forge the Banner of the Northern Star, summon Harbingers and Arch Angels).
  - **Standard**: worth it early. It delays routs.
  - **Centurion**: the cheap leader for formations. **Legatus** when the morale bonus matters.
  - **Hydras**: need a high-morale commander and Body Ethereal + Luck. Their poison cloud hurts
    our own troops unless they have poison resistance.
- **Summons:**
  - **Contact Harbinger** (Conjuration 6, S4: an astral Arch Theurg or a boosted one) is the best national summon: it leads Great Eagles and
    breaks walls instantly.
  - **Angelic Host** (Conjuration 7) breaks us into F4 with astral gems.
  - Contact Lar (Conjuration 5) is the key mage if we have nature access.
  - Hamadryad and Angel of Fury aren't worth it.
- **National items:**
  - Gloves of the Gladiator (Construction 2): four attacks on one target, best on a big thug
    (our Titan);
  - Hydra Skin Armor (Construction 6): +10% regeneration.

**For our game:**
- Hortus is E5 N5, so **the god can Contact Lar** (Conjuration 5, N1, 16 nature gems; checked in the inspector) once he wakes. A Lar is W1 E1 N2 plus a random pick, sacred, ethereal and stealthy; it gives Pythium earth, nature and water mages for communions and site searches.
- Keep Emerald Guards on Theurgs if an assassin nation is near.
- Retiarius + Fog Warriors is a combination to test once Alteration/Evocation are in.

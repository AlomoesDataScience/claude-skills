## Description

On the dark and densely forested island of Lanka, Rakshasas, demon ogres from an earlier era, still linger. Gifted with tremendous strength, they once ought the Devatas of Kailasa, but since then the most powerful of the Rakshasas have left this world for the Nether Realms. The remaining Rakshasas have captured and enslaved the monkey people of Kailasa and made them serve as warriors, hunters or food. The Rakshasas had no use for the peaceful White Ones and Kala-Mukhas, Rakshasa halfbreeds, lead the monkey people of Lanka. Since the enslavement of the apes, the ancient conflict} between the Devatas of Kailasa and the Rakshasas of Lanka has been renewed. Rakshasas have an affinity for blood magic and in times of strife they are drawn to the blood of innocents.

## National features

- **Race:** Demon ogres, apes and undead, prefers Heat scale +2
- **Military:** Strong Rakshasa infantry, missile units and light infantry, reanimated apes
- **Magic:** Air, Blood, Death, Glamour, Nature
- **Priests:** Medium. National priests can reanimate the dead.
- **Dominion:** Blood hunts give more blood slaves in turmoil and less in order
- **Scales & Blesses:** Turmoil limit +1, Heat limit +1
- **Buildings:** Standard Forts

## Description

Tir na n'Og is a blessed isle surrounded by whispering mists. It is the home of the Tuatha, a highly magical race descended from the Nemedians who once warred with the Fomorians. The Tuatha have made themselves lords and masters over the Fir Bolg, a lesser race descended from the same Nemedian ancestors. With the aid of the Fir Bolg, the Tuatha attacked the Fomorian giants and almost succeeded where the Nemedians failed, but eventually the Fomorians proved too powerful and the Tuatha were driven back to their Blessed Isle. The Tuatha and their Sidhe descendants have turned to crafts, poetry and magical arts, but some have chosen the path of war and have become warriors and generals of great renown. The Fir Bolg, inferior to the Tuatha but still far superior to ordinary men, compose much of the population and serve as farmers and lowly warriors.

## National features

- **Race:** Tuatha, Sidhe, Fir Bolg, illusions
- **Military:** Fir Bolg infantry, Sidhe infantry, sacred Tuatha warriors
- **Magic:** Glamour, Nature, Water, some Air and Earth
- **Priests:** Average
- **Scales & Blesses:** Luck limit +1, Magic limit +1

## From the community wiki (illwiki, distilled 2026-10-28; the user's MP nation)

- **Sources:**
  - The Dom6 page (`wiki.py show tir-na-n-og-ea`) has the race bonuses and stat tables.
  - The **Dom5** page (`wiki.py show dom5/tir-na-n-og-ea`) has the player guide. Costs differ in
    Dom6; the roles hold.
- **Dom6 race bonuses:**
  - **Fir Bolg** (size 3): +3 hp, +3 MR, +1 morale, +2 str, +2 att, +3 def, +1 combat speed,
    +4 map move.
  - **Sidhe / Tuatha**: +3 hp, +4 MR, +2 morale, +2 str, +3 att, +3 def, +2 prec, +1 combat
    speed; darkvision 50, stealthy, glamour, forest survival, **iron vulnerability 1**.
  - Tir na n'Og has Luck limit +1 and Magic limit +1.
- **What the nation is:** an elf nation built on stealth, glamour, assassination and raiding, with
  strong **air and nature** magic and cheap research (Fir Bolg Druids).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - expensive elite troops;
  - little magic diversity (air and nature);
  - **the elves die to missiles and evocations**: missiles ignore defence and glamour.
- **Unit roles (Dom5 commentary):**
  - **Tuatha Warrior** (capital-only sacred): magic weapons, very high attack and defence,
    average protection, near-human hp. Cleans up most troops. **Weak to ranged attacks.**
    - Good blesses: resistances or protection to cover that weakness, or minor stat boosts
      (attack, defence, Blood Surge).
  - **Fir Bolg Warrior** (axe or spear + javelin): better than most light infantry, but 7
    protection and no helmet. Axes add damage; spears are the better chaff.
  - **Fir Bolg Slinger**: "unimpressive", short range, low damage. Siege chaff, or worth more
    with strength or missile buffs.
  - **Sidhe Warrior**: Fir Bolg stats plus stealth and glamour. Makes an all-stealth army
    possible, at almost twice the gold.
  - **Sidhe Lord**: commander, A2 caster and **one of the most cost-effective thugs in the
    game**: a Frost Brand, a Vine Shield and Personal Mistform kill most PD thanks to their
    huge defence.
    - Raid with Barkskin, Air Shield, Mistform and Personal Regeneration; Cloud Trapeze
      (Enchantment 4) drops them behind the lines.
    - Don't overgear them: low hp and protection.
  - **Baobhan Sidhe**: A2, stealth 85, assassin. With gems she summons Air Elementals to kill
    commanders. One of the best assassins in the game.
  - **Bean Sidhe**: a random path each. The earth ones are the best site-searchers, water the
    only good W access, N2 casts Swarm.
  - **Tuatha Sorceress** (capital-only): A3 N2, up to A4/N3 with randoms. The main high caster:
    Storm, Arrow Fend, Mass Flight, Fog Warriors, Howl, Mass Protection.
  - **Ri**: not worth it.
  - **Fir Bolg Druid**: cheap researcher.
- **Early game (guide):**
  - Expand with **6-8 Tuatha** or **~25 Fir Bolg under a Champion**.
  - **Two unequipped Sidhe Lords** take weak to medium independent provinces.
  - **Avoid elephant and shaman/tribe (Tangle Vines) provinces.**
  - First fort on turn 7-8.
- **Research (guide):**
  - Conjuration 5 for elemental powers and Air Elementals, *or* Conjuration 3 + Evocation 5
    for Storm + Thunder Strike (Baobhan / Sidhe Lord A2 batteries under the Sorceress's Storm).
    Which first depends on the neighbours' magic weapon / shock resistance blesses.
  - Then Alteration 7 (Wooden Warriors, Mass Protection, Fog Warriors, Mother Oak) and
    Enchantment 4 (Cloud Trapeze, Seeking Arrow).
- **Midgame is their strongest phase:** press hard.
  - Globals: **Mother Oak** (Alteration 5, N5) and Perpetual Storm (Evocation 6, A5).
- **National items:**
  - **Shillelagh** (Construction 2-3): Luck + a Sprite that casts Elf Shot.
  - **Gossamer Cloth**: the army is reported smaller.
- **For the user's current plan** (Enchantment 2 → Thaumaturgy 3, Haruspex/Gnome Lore):
  - **Sling damage is low**, so Envenom Arrows (+5 AN poison when the missile wounds) matters
    more than strength buffs. It's still unverified whether Envenom works on slings.
  - The guide's own research priorities are Conjuration and Evocation (Storm, Thunder Strike,
    Air Elementals).

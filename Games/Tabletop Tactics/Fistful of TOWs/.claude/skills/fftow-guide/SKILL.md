# A Fistful of TOWs 3 - starter guide

Status 2026-10-29: rules outline and introduction read; the user's lists skimmed. Not played.

Files (in `Fist Full of TOW/`; `Fist Full of TOW.7z` is the archive): `Fist Full of Tow.pdf`
(462 pages: the rules are the first ~180, then equipment data and army lists) and its `.txt`.

## What it is (Claude's summary)

Fast big-battle miniatures rules for modern mechanized war: turns of about 15 minutes of play,
**troop quality** affecting every system, no pre-plotted artillery, simple command without leader
stands, and data for nearly every major weapon system since the 1930s, plus rules to rate your
own equipment and a point system for balancing scenarios.

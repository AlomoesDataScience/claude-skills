# Star Fleet Universe: Galactic Conquest - starter guide

Status 2026-10-29: rulebook (Version 5C) read in contents, general rules, time scale and
combat basics. Not played yet. The PDF is the user's purchased copy (their order watermark is on
every page): keep it local, never share or quote it at length.

Files: `Star_Fleet_Galactic_Conquest.pdf` and `.txt`. PDF page = printed page + 1. Rules are
numbered `(A1.10)`: search `manual.py grep Star_Fleet_Galactic_Conquest.txt "\(B11\.1[0-9]\)"`.

## What it is (Claude's summary)

Not a board game on its own: a campaign framework for the Star Fleet Universe (the Star Trek-based
setting of Star Fleet Battles and Federation & Empire - the user owns F&E as a physical game). Players run empires and write **orders for squadrons** as admirals; a
**gamemaster (GM)** resolves movement and battles, by statistical combat (or by playing a battle
out in Star Fleet Battles). Each turn is six months of twelve two-week segments.

- **Orders** (A9): a strict format per squadron; the admiral sets a **battle intensity** (seven
  levels from Suicidal to Evasion) and a **disengagement intensity** (the % loss at which ships
  break off for the rest of the turn). At least one round is fought before anyone may withdraw;
  legendary admirals may look first and refuse battle.
- **Combat** (A10, appendix 1): each unit has attack and defence factors; a unit reduced to half
  its defence loses half its attack. Interception stops squadrons advancing into enemy space until
  a battle is won.
- **Economics** (B): economic points from a base economic value, trade routes, commerce and
  off-map areas; morale and rebellion, economic exhaustion, a galactic bank, Orion pirates,
  surrender. A crippled empire's income halves.
- **Strategic operations** (C): repair, minefields, sensors and cloaking, hidden units,
  espionage, R&D, X-ships, fighters and gunboats, expeditionary fleets, alert levels.
- **Movement** (D): reaction movement, high-powered, double-time and trans-warp moves, fleet
  formation and stacking. Merchant marine (E), starting navy purchase (Z).
- Victory (A11) is judged each turn rather than only at the end.

## How Claude helps

Claude can act as the **GM**: keep the empires' status sheets, read the players' orders, resolve
moves and statistical combat with the dice openly logged, and write battle reports coded by hex
and segment (A2). Or Claude advises one admiral. Ask the user which.

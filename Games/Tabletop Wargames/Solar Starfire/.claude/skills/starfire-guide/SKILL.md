# Solar Starfire - starter guide

Status 2026-10-29: rulebook (SSF-201307) read in outline, "how to use these rules" and the
Space Master's monthly processing. Not played yet. A purchased rulebook: keep it local.

Files: `Solar_Starfire.pdf` (429 pages, bookmarks) and `.txt`. **PDF page = printed page + 18**

`[C4.06]`: `manual.py grep Solar_Starfire.txt "^ *L7\.0[0-9]"`.

## What it is (Claude's summary)

Starfire (since 1979) is two games in one book:
- **Tactical** (sections A-H, plus appendices AA technology and EE tables): ship-to-ship battles
  on a hex map. Ships are boxes of systems on control sheets; engine rooms give movement,
  facing matters, warp points join star systems (transits, assaults), damage knocks out systems in
  order, sensors and detection, bases and planetary defence centres, small craft squadrons, crew
  grade, and ship design (H).
- **Strategic campaign** (J-V): run an interstellar empire month by month - set up the empire
  (K), population and industrial units and income (L), construction (M), R&D and tech levels (N),
  command and communications lag (P), strategic movement (Q), survey of warp lines and systems
  (R), leaders (S, optional), politics and first contact with other races (T), intelligence (U),
  campaign combat (V).
- **Space Master** (W-Z, DD): builds the galaxy (W), processes every player's monthly turn
  (X: check income, maintenance, designs, construction, R&D, resolve combat, roll R&D and
  politics, send results), and runs the **non-player races** (Y, generated with their own
  tendencies). Campaign ideas in DD; play advice and a full example month in CC (p321+).

# Rise and Decline of the Third Reich - starter guide

Status 2026-10-29: the fan 4.5-edition rules read in introduction, victory and section list. The
user owns the physical game (map and counters). Not played yet.

Files: `3r4-5th-DQB-v7.pdf` (71 pages, the **newer one: use this**) and `3r4_5th_v4.pdf` (an
earlier version of the same rules; about 3000 lines differ), each with a `.txt`. DQB entries
(official question-and-answer rulings) are grey in the PDF. Rules are numbered: `manual.py grep
3r4-5th-DQB-v7.txt "^ *18\.3"`. The rules say they are free only for owners of the game.

## What it is (Claude's summary)

All of WWII in Europe and North Africa on one hex map, 2-6 players (each major power can be its
own player: Germany, Italy, Britain, France, Russia, USA). The economy is **BRPs**: every attack,
unit, declaration of war and repair costs them, and they grow or shrink each year with conquests
and strategic warfare. Turns are three months; within a turn each side takes a player turn, the
side with more BRPs usually moving first. Per player turn, each power chooses for each of the
three **fronts** (Western, Eastern, Mediterranean) an **Offensive** option (costs BRPs, allows
attacks), an **Attrition** option (cheap grinding) or **Pass**. Between winter and spring the
**year start sequence** resolves strategic warfare (U-boats, bombers), construction and BRP
levels. New players should start with the 1944 scenario, the simplest.

Winning (2.0): mostly by red objective hexes held at the end (14 Western, 14 Eastern, plus the
Mediterranean ones), or by how long a nation survives, or by when the last Axis power falls.
